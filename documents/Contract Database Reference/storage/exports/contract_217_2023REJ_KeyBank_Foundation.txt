Before You Begin
Introduction
 
Thank you for accepting KeyBank Foundation’s invitation to apply for a grant. We see
clear alignment between our two organizations and are interested in the potential for a
partnership to positively impact in our communities.
KeyBank Foundation is a nonprofit charitable foundation, funded by KeyCorp, KeyBank National
Association (“KeyBank”), and their affiliates. KeyBank Foundation supports organizations and
programs that prepare individuals for thriving futures. As a corporate neighbor, our mission is
advanced through our three funding priorities, Neighbors,Education andWorkforce with a focus
on advancingsocio-economic,racial, andenvironmental equity.
 
   
Funding Priorities
 
Advancing social, racial, and environmental equity by investing in low-to-moderate
income individuals and families. KeyBank Foundation seeks to invest in solutions which
address imbalanced social systems in order to create equitable, thriving communities.
1. Neighbors: Grants that support Neighbors create prosperity through advancing economic
inclusion; communities and housing that are safe, healthy, affordable and stable; and the
development of small businesses. Grant focus on access to affordable housing services;
families that are economically self-sufficient; safe and stable communities; and small business
growth and success.
2. Education: Grants that support Education improve intellectual capital through higher
education, vocational and secondary education graduation and completion. We provide
students the chance to prepare for fulfilling careers through access to high quality education
and support for their academic success.
3. Workforce : Grants that support Workforce enhance economic vitality through jobs creation,
attainment, retention and workforce development. Grants focus on equipping adults with the
necessary skills, education and capabilities to meet current and future local employment
demands.

 Organization's Mission Statement 
 
The mission of Community Action Lehigh Valley is to improve the quality of life in the Lehigh Valley

 Organization’s Background Summary 
 Brief history & description of activities 
 
Community Action Lehigh Valley (CALV) has a rich history of serving the community and addressing
social needs. Founded in 1965, CALV has been at the forefront of promoting self-sufficiency,
economic development, and community empowerment.
CALV's initiatives span different areas of focus to provide programs and services that meet basic
social needs while encouraging self-sufficiency. They offer food assistance through the Second
Harvest Food Bank of the Lehigh Valley and provide short and long-term shelter for homeless

families at the Sixth Street Shelter. Their housing counseling agency, Community Action Financial
Services, promotes self-sufficiency and financial literacy through education, counseling, and
assistance related to homeownership, predatory lending, and mortgage foreclosure. The
Weatherization Program addresses home heating problems by providing counseling and
weatherization services for residences.
CALV is also committed to social justice and racial equity. The Campaign for Racial and Ethnic Justice
promotes inclusion, equity, and justice through community dialogue, leadership development, and
expanding access to resources. They actively work to cultivate relationships, networks, and
opportunities for underrepresented communities. Furthermore, CALV runs programs like SHE and
Generation Next, which support youth in reaching their educational potential.
To support economic and community development, CALV operates subsidiary corporations such as
the Community Action Development Corporation of Allentown and Bethlehem. These entities work
directly with residents to revitalize their communities and promote growth.
Overall, Community Action Lehigh Valley has been a driving force in addressing social needs,
promoting self-sufficiency, and fostering economic and community development, with a commitment
to eliminating poverty.

What impact will this project have on the beneficiaries or the total community?
 
The mission of SHE is to create an educational and inspirational environment in which girls build
self-esteem, are empowered to change the dynamics in their personal relationships and the greater
community, and seize control of their futures, ensuring healthy, fulfilling, and self-sufficient lives.
Girls have the power to shape their futures and their community for the better. Girls have the power
to make change. All they need is an opportunity to harness their voices and embrace their collective
strength, and they will change the world! SHE works with girls to develop a strong, supportive peer
network while honing the skills they need to find inner strength in themselves and develop into
leaders in their community.
This is accomplished through interactive games, discussions, field trips, and student-led projects, in
which girls develop the skills and knowledge necessary to navigate the process of career planning
and higher education preparation, develop a healthy attitude toward all relationships—personal,
peer, romantic, sexual, and family—and harness the power of girls’ voices in changing the world
around them.
SHE currently operates in 3 elementary schools and 2 middle schools in the Bethlehem Area School
District and serves 80 students. In the Allentown School District, SHE currently operates in two
middle schools and serves 54 students . In the 2022-2023 school year, the SHE program is projected
to serve 100-140 students.
