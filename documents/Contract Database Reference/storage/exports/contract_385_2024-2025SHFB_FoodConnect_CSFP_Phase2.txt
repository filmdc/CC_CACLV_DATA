Purpose: <p><strong>Purpose</strong></p><p>This Service Agreement establishes the relationship between SHFB and FoodConnect, a nonprofit organization focused on technology and logistics services in relation to hunger relief. This agreement is in relation to funding from a <a href="http://contracts.caclv.org/contracts/375">Feeding PA grant</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>FoodConnect will be delivering senior boxes directly to seniors' homes. FoodConnect will provide detailed distribution reports for use in reporting back to Feeding PA.</p><p>The date of this agreement is from July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2025.</p><p>FoodConnect will bill monhly on actual delivery volumes, according to the below schedule.</p><p>The relationship between SHFB and FoodConnect is being implemented in Phases, with the first phase being complete upon June 30, 2024. Additional phases will focus on participant expansion in Lehigh and Northampton Counties initially. A contract amendment signed November 2024 expands the scope of deliveries into Carbon and Monroe Counties.</p><p>Expansion scope and terms will be mutually agreed upon by SHFB and FoodConnect.</p><p>The tiered pricing model below is applicable to deliveries made in Lehigh and Northampton Counties only. Updated tiered pricing and contract addendum will be issued as necessary..</p><figure class="image"><img src="http://10.55.0.10/storage/1162/image.png"></figure><p>Tiered pricing for Carbon and Monroe Counties:</p><figure class="image"><img src="http://10.55.0.10/storage/1275/image.png"></figure><p>Questions regarding this service agreement should be directed to Kate Colyer, Regional Program Director, Northeast at kate@foodconnectgroup.com.</p>
