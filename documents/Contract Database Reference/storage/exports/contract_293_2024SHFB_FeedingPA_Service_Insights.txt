Overview of Organization 
Second Harvest Food Bank of the Lehigh Valley and Northeast PA is a program of Community Action Lehigh 
Valley, the region’s leading anti-poverty organization, and a member of Feeding America – the nation’s 
largest charitable food network. Second Harvest serves Lehigh, Northampton, Carbon, Monroe, Pike, and 
Wayne counties.  
Last year, Second Harvest distributed 14.7 million pounds of food to our network of 200+ nonprofit 
agencies (food pantries, schools, meal centers, shelters, childcare agencies, senior centers, veterans 
organizations), a 44% increase from the previous year. Included in this total is 6.7 million pounds of fresh 
produce, perishable protein, and fresh dairy products (46% of total distribution). Annually, we rescue more 
than 8 million pounds of food from ending up in a landfill.  
On average, the Second Harvest network serves more than 87,000 people each month - a 52% increase 
from last year. The pantries within the Second Harvest network are operated almost solely through the 
work of community volunteers. Our food distribution is a model of neighbors helping neighbors.  
In addition to food distribution to our network, Second Harvest operates several programs aimed at 
improving the health of our community and working toward a more just and equitable local food system. 
We administer the Pennsylvania Senior Food Box Program (PASB). PASB works to improve the health of 
low-income seniors by supplementing their diets with shelf-stable nutritious foods. We work to 
supplement the boxes with healthy, fresh items when possible. Currently, Second Harvest provides senior 
food boxes to 2,350 seniors across 49 sites each month. 
Our MilitaryShare program works “to serve those who served us” by providing access to nutritious food, 
with a goal of improving health outcomes and easing financial strain among low-income active duty, 
reserve, and retired military members and their families. Our MilitaryShare program provides 500+ 
military families with a share of fresh and shelf-stable food each month.  
Our SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) outreach coordinators work with local county 
offices of Health and Human Services to offer SNAP application assistance. Our SNAP coordinators offer 
dedicated personal application support to ensure that all eligible participants receive needed benefits. 
We offer a dynamic community-based nutrition education program, which includes free cooking classes 
and demonstrations for parents, kids, and childcare providers, where participants learn about cooking, 
meal prep, grocery shopping, food budgeting, and nutrition. Through our Healthy Pantry Initiative (HPI), a 
program of Feeding Pennsylvania, our HPI Coordinator works with pantry staff and volunteers to 
encourage consumption of nutritious and fresh foods, and also provides direct nutrition education to 
pantry clients.  
Additionally, Second Harvest operates The Seed Farm, a 42-acre training farm. Because there can be no 
access to nourishing food without farms, The Seed Farm works to support food production at all levels,

Project Description 
It is our priority to move into more data-informed decision making. Electronic data collection at the pantry-
level will lead to a better understanding of the people we serve and will ensure we are able to keep the 
needs of our participants at the forefront of our strategic planning, program development, and food 
distribution efforts. We also recognize that we will be excluded from future funding opportunities if we 
are unable to supply the demographic data required by funders. Developing and standardizing our data 
collection mechanisms is essential.  
Our primary goals for pantry-level electronic data collection include: 
• Obtain an unduplicated count of the people served by the food bank; 
• Gain an understanding of the demographic composition of the people utilizing our pantry 
network; 
• Facilitate effective culturally relevant and health conscience food and nutrition information; and 
• Streamline reporting for TEFAP requirements and other government and non-government 
programs. 
 
During fiscal year 2023, an intern worked with Second Harvest staff to develop internal communication 
strategies; gauged interest among a pilot group of pantries; and worked with pantries to complete the 
process that would allow them to gain access to the test site (data sharing agreement, client privacy 
agreement). A draft workplan has been developed, along with an SOP and toolkit for food bank staff use. 
Basic partner agency training tools have been created to assist with onboarding.  
Our Community Programs Coordinator will build upon the work done by our intern and will oversee the 
rollout of our electronic data collection initiative, starting with a cohort of five (5) agencies.  
Our rollout plan aligns with Feeding America’s recommended strategy. The Second Harvest network is 
comprised of 200+ partner agencies. Our goal is to onboard 60 agencies within our first year of 
implementation (January – December 2024).  
Phase one of rollout will include pantries that offer a full-choice grocery model serving the entire 
household, participate in our retail recovery program, participate in our Healthy Pantry Initiative, and the 
OrderAhead grocery ordering system.  
The second phase of rollout will include pantries that do not offer full-choice and/or programs that serve 
specific populations – kids, veterans, college students.  
Pantries will be ranked by number of people served, with the pantries serving the greatest number of 
people given priority. Priority will also be given to agencies that receive support from Second Harvest 
through funders that require data-driven reporting measures (exm: United Way). This will ensure that we 
are able to satisfy donor requirements to keep future funding secure, ultimately allowing us to expand our 
reach and serve more neighbors.

Additionally, our Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP), which serves seniors in-need, currently 
utilizes Link2Feed through our contract with Feeding Pennsylvania. We provide CSFP food to seniors at 49 
sites. The Community Programs Coordinator will provide electronic data collection oversight to all CSFP 
sites, in addition to pantry sites.  
It is our goal to onboard all partner agencies to participate in electronic data collection within four years 
of initial implementation. The Community Programs Coordinator will dedicate about ¾ of their time to 
electronic data collection for the initial four years. Once the entirety of our partner network has been set-
up, the maintenance of the program will be transitioned to agency relations staff. 
 
Timeline of Activities for Grant Period 
The Community Programs Coordinator job description has been posted and we anticipate hiring to fill the 
role by mid-January 2024. 
Following an orientation period, the Community Programs Coordinator will immediately review the 
research and data that was gathered and the materials and tools that were created by our intern, and will 
work through the Feeding America checklists to create a framework for building out our electronic data 
collection pilot program.  
The Coordinator will conduct stakeholder meetings and interviews with our pantry network and will 
identify the first cohort of five (5) partner agencies. 
The Coordinator will onboard and train the first cohort, taking steps to learn best practices and hone 
training techniques. Additional partner agencies will be continually added into the program, with the goal 
of reaching 60 participating partners by the end of December 2024.  
 
Measurement of Success 
Success will be measured by: 
1) Our ability to confidently report on the number of unduplicated people served, and 
2) The consistent, complete, and timely submission of monthly reporting by our agencies.  
 
Budget and Plan for Sustainability 
Second Harvest Food Bank respectfully requests $50,000 from Feeding Pennsylvania to support the partial 
salary and fringe benefits of the Community Programs Coordinator to build out and implement electronic 
data collection at the pantry-level. The Community Programs Coordinator will dedicate ¾ of their time to 
the electronic data collection initiative (approximately .75 FTE). 
Second Harvest is sustained by a multi-faceted development strategy. Funding comes from corporations, 
foundations, government entities, and individual donors.  Second Harvest has a 40-year history of meeting 
our fundraising goals and supporting our programs, which will include electronic data collection.
