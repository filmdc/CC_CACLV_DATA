Univest Bank and Trust Co. (FEIN 23-1171580) is pleased to partner with the Community Action 
Development Corporation of Bethlehem (CADCB) by pledging to support the Neighborhood 
Partnership Program. I have enclosed a copy of the NPP Business/Community Partnership 
Agreement between Univest Bank and Trust Co. and CADCB which supports the 6-year 
commitment of $100,000 to this program by Univest Bank and Trust Co.  
 
Under the commitment, Univest Bank and Trust Co. has pledged to provide CADBC with a 
donation of $100,000 for fiscal year 2021-22, which represents year one of our six-year 
commitment. Payment shall be made by Univest Bank and Trust Co. during the NPP contract 
period of July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022. This commitment is contingent upon the approval of 
CADCB for the Neighborhood Partnership Program of the PA Department of Community and 
Economic Development. I also understand that in exchange for this contribution of $100,000, 
Univest Bank and Trust Co. will receive an 80% tax credit from the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania.  
 
We look forward to a partnership with your organization for the continued revitalization of 
south Bethlehem.
