*Funding Purpose - Program/Project
Description
The proposed housing project of Community Action will provide comprehensive
housing strategies that aim to promote home ownership, preserve and improve
the quality of affordable housing, and increase the affordable housing stock. We
focus resources on our service areas (City of Allentown, City of Bethlehem,
Northampton County, and Lehigh County) to undertake housing counseling,
housing acquisition/rehab/resale, owner-occupied housing rehabilitation, and
façade improvement, enhancing housing stability, overall health, and quality of
life of the families we assist.
Community Action's Housing Counseling Program (HCP) provides low- to
moderate-income individuals and families with a continuum of coordinated
financial counseling services in one location in order to encourage and assist
them to build personal assets, to increase and preserve homeownership, and to
develop neighborhood wealth for the benefit of the whole community.
Community Action Homes (CAH) works to preserve affordable housing and
expand housing stock for low- to moderate-income families in Lehigh Valley. CAH
strives to bring positive changes to the lives, living conditions, and opportunities
of Lehigh Valley residents by helping low- to moderate-income households
acquire and remain in quality and affordable homes.
Funding from Wells Fargo will support HCP and CAH in their goals to create 2
affordable housing units for low-to-moderate-income (LMI) households to
become homeowners, assist 115 LMI homeowners in preserving their homes
through owner-occupied housing rehab and foreclosure prevention services, and
provide pre-purchase education and counseling to support potential
homebuyers to purchase their first homes during the project period.

Description of LMI/SB populations
served:
Of HCP's participants, approximately 71% are low- to moderate-income and face
the following: credit issues, inadequate savings for down payment & closing costs,
& lack education regarding the home-buying process and possible foreclosure.
Homeownership & foreclosure counseling provide education & empowerment to
help maximize resources, build assets & gain skills to be self-sufficient.
Of CAH's participants, 95% are LMI and face the following: lack of financial access
to rehabilitate their existing owner-occupied residence and lack of affordable
homeownership options. CAH's housing rehabilitation provides housing security
and improves the quality of life for LMI residents by reducing the stress of
hazardous living conditions and economic burden of home repair. Repairs may
also improve the energy efficiency of the homes, resulting in cost savings.
Acquisition-rehab-resale provides low- to moderate-income individuals the access
to purchase a quality, safe home at an affordable price.
