I am pleased to inform you that your request for a Neighborhood Assistance Neighborhood Partnership

transmit to Community Action Development Corporation of the Lehigh Valley	, an offer for a

capitalized terms used herein shall have the meanings assigned in the Law, regulations, guidelines, and 
policies promulgated thereunder.

Coordinator:

totaling a maximum of 	$650,000.00 shall be accepted from project donors between July 	1	, 	2023 
and June 	30	, 	2024	, and all funds must be spent by September 	30	, 	2024	.
• Tax Credits awarded for this project must be supplied solely for contributions to carry out the

Tax Credits to the Department. The Department reserves the right to recollect and redistribute the
