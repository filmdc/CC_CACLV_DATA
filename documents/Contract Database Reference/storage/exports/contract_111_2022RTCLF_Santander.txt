2022 Santander Grant Agreement 
 
Grantor: Santander Bank, N.A. (“Grantor”) 
 
Grantee(s): RISING TIDE COMMUNITY LOAN FUND (“Grantee”) 
 
Grant Amount: $10,000.00 
 
Grant Term: This Agreement is entered into as of December 1, 2022 (the “Effective Date”) and 
shall expire on November 30, 2023 (the “Expiration Date”). 
 
Reporting Schedule: Within six (6) months of the date of execution of this grant agreement by 
the parties and within forty-five (45) days of the end date of the grant term, and/or upon request 
of the Grantor.   
 
Grant Purpose: General operating supporting community needs in the wake of the COVID-19 
pandemic, the Small Business Opportunity Loan Fund, the Bethlehem Small Business Loan Fund, 
microloans, lines of credit, marketing and technical assistance and program administration. (the 
“Grant Purpose”). 
 
This is a legally binding agreement (“Agreement”) by and between Grantor and Grantee(s).  By 
entering into this Agreement, Grantee acknowledges that the Grant is awarded by the Grantor to 
Grantee subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement. 
 
Terms and Conditions 
 
1. Use of Funds.  The Grant may be used only for the Grant Purpose.  Grantee accepts 
responsibility for complying with this Agreement’s terms and conditions and will exercise full 
control over the Grant and the expenditure of the Grant.  Grantee may not use any Grant 
funds to lobby or otherwise attempt to influence legislation, to influence the outcome of any 
public election, to carry on, directly or indirectly, any voter registration drive, or to engage in 
any illegal activity.  Any significant changes in the purpose for which Grant funds are spent 
during Grant Term must be approved in writing by Grantor before the funds are spent.  The 
Grantor may request that Grantee return any unexpended amount of the Grant.        
    
2. Payment Terms. The Grant will be paid within forty-five (45) days of Grantor’s receipt of the 
Agreement executed by the Grantee.  
 
3. Reporting.  An interim grant report (“Interim Grant Report”) shall be provided within 6 months 
of the start of the grant term. A final grant report (“Final Grant Report”) shall be provided within 
forty-five (45) days of the conclusion of the grant term.  The Final Grant Report shall be in 
form and substance satisfactory to the Grantor, to include inter alia the following information: 
  
(a) The use of the donated funds and the progress made toward achieving the objectives for 
which the Grant was made;  
 
(b) The impact of the funds granted (how this Grant has made a difference);  
 
(c) Whether or not the funds were diverted from the purpose of the Grant;

(d) A financial report detailing how the funds were expended, including a breakdown of the

(e) The portion of the Grant, if any, reported by the Grantee as tax exempt and non-tax 
exempt; and  
 
(f) If the project received publicity, include a copy or description in the Grant Report.  
 
If the Grant is not fully expended by the Grantee within twelve (12) months of the Grantee’s

thereafter, until completion of the use of the granted funds, or until Grantor advises the 
Grantee, in writing, that such reports will no longer be required. The purpose of these 
report(s) is to permit the Grantor to track the status and use of the Grant as well as to allow 
Grantor to meet any state or federal obligations and/or other commitments it may have. The 
form of any such reporting shall be determined and agreed to in writing by the parties. 
 
Grantee will maintain its record of expenditure from the Grant, as well as copies of the Interim 
and Final Grant Report(s) for at least four (4) years after completion of the use of the Grant.  
Grantee will make its books and records available to the Grantor at reasonable times during 
such period.  
 
4. Representation and Warranty Regarding Tax Status. Grantee is an organization that is 
currently recognized by the Internal Revenue Service (the “IRS”) as a public charity under 
sections 501(c)(3), 501(c)(4), 501(c)(6) and/or 509(a)(1) or 509(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986, as amended (the “Code”), and Grantee will inform Grantor  immediately of any 
change in, or IRS proposed or actual revocation of its tax status as a public charity.  Grantee 
acknowledges and agrees that Grantor is entering into this Agreement in reliance on this 
representation and that such representation shall continue through the completion date of the 
Grant.

the Grantor is committed to equal opportunities, inclusion and mutual respect for all.

Categories”), with respect to employment, membership, and/or provision of services.  This 
support includes direct grants, matching gifts, employee volunteer grants and any other 
capacity in which the Grantor may provide assistance.

7. Sub-Grantees Grantee agrees that it will obtain Grantor’s written approval prior to retaining

8. Publications; License. Grantee will allow the Grantor to review and approve the text of any 
proposed publicity concerning the Grant prior to its release.  Grantee grants to Grantor an
