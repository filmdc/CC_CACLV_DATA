Purpose: <p>Pike County has awarded SHFB a CDBG grant for $34,508.00 for the purchase of fresh food for Pike County. This is a <strong>reimbursable grant</strong> with the grant period running from <strong>May 9, 2024 - August 31, 2025</strong>. Food will be distributed to partner agencies serving Pike county.&nbsp;</p><p>Fresh food includes dairy, meat, eggs, fruits, and vegetables. Whenever possible, fresh food shall be procured from farmers located in Pike County.</p><p>Food pantries serviced with CDBG funds must prepare and submit a letter on letterhead stating they will collect a Self Declaration of Need form from each individual served and that no food or non-food items purchased through this grant will be distributed to individuals who have not completed a Self Declaration of need form or who are deemed to be ineligible due to income. A template for this letter can be found in Exhibit B of the contract attached.</p><p>Reports are due bi-monthly, according to the chart below.
