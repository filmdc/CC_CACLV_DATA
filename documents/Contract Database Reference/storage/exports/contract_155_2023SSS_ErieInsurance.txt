Describe your project, including its purpose and detailed
information on its goals (max 2,000 characters)
Sixth Street Shelter provides safe, short-term housing for low-income 
families with at least one child under the age of 18 who have become 
homeless. The property has 25 private apartments, a Family Resource 
Center, playground, and full-choice food pantry for residents. For many 
families, the 60- to 90-day stay at the Shelter enables them to begin to 
stabilize their income and address the problems that led to their 
homelessness. Families come to the Shelter for various reasons: low-
paying jobs, lack of affordable housing, mental and/or physical health 
crises, domestic violence, and lack of access to reliable transportation, 
healthcare, and/or childcare. The Shelter provides each family with a 
furnished apartment and supportive services, including individualized 
case management. Staff work to connect them with the necessary 
resources to address their individual needs, such as financial literacy 
and parenting classes, access to childcare and healthcare, and mental 
health support. During their residency, the family develops a Family 
Service Plan with their caseworker, setting household, budgeting, 
educational, and vocational goals and seeking suitable housing and 
employment.  It costs $3,300 per month to provide a family with a 
furnished apartment and supportive services; a $9,900 grant would 
sponsor a family through a 3-month stay at the Sixth Street Shelter as 
they work toward self-sufficiency.'
In our current fiscal year, Sixth Street Shelter has set the following 
performance targets: 1) At least 100 families will reside at the Shelter 
and develop Family Service Plans based on their housing, educational 
and financial goals; 2) 50% of families will save money toward rent or 
a security deposit upon leaving the Shelter; 3) 75% of families will 
achieve their Family Service Plan goals; 4) 100% of families will create 
a written budget to understand credit and money management as a 
part of their goal plan; 5) 40% of families will move into affordable 
housing upon leaving.
