Strategic Context
   
Commitment to Equity
To achieve our vision of an America where no one is hungry, we are committed to equity, ensuring fair treatment, access, opportunity, and advancement for all people. We will identify and work towards solutions to eliminate social, structural, and systemic inequities that contribute to food insecurity, with a focus on communities that have been historically disadvantaged and/or adversely impacted by racial and/or geographical inequities. While there are many components to equity, racial inequity has resulted in poorer outcomes for communities of color, including higher rates of food insecurity and poverty, and worse health outcomes.
