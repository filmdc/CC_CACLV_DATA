Purpose: <p>This agreement between SHFB and the PA Dept of Agriculture (PDA), establishes the terms and conditions for participation in The Emergency Food Assistance Program (TEFAP).&nbsp;</p><p>The program directs SHFB in the receipt of food provided by PDA/USDA and equitable distribution to partner agencies (food pantries). This contract gives SHFB the right to continue TEFAP contracts with the Counties in Eastern PA.</p><p>Food designated as TEFAP must be accounted for separately from other food in the warehouse.</p><p>This is a <strong>reimbursable grant</strong>. Invoices submitted to the PDA must have a unique invoice number. Invoices must be submitted along with quarterly Inventory Utilization Report Forms.</p><p>SHFB may not charge any partner agency a shared maintenance fee. PDA may elect to reimburse expenses; SHFB is responsible for providing an Inventory Utilization Report Form quarterly to the PDA.</p><p>Additional TEFAP bonus streams may be awarded during the contract period. These funds may be disbursed without an additional contract.</p>
