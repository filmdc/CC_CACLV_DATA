The purpose of this letter is to affirm Waste Management’s commitment to support the 
Slate Belt Rising Neighborhood Partnership Program for the upcoming 2022-2023 fiscal 
year. I have enclosed a copy of the newly drafted NPP Business/Community Partnership 
Agreement between Waste Management (FEIN # 25- 1232336) and the Community 
Action Committee of the Lehigh Valley (CALV) to be executed by representatives of 
both organizations. This agreement supports Waste Management’s six-year commitment 
to this program.  
 
This letter represents year one of our six-year commitment for $50,000 per year. Per the 
agreement, for the 2022-2023 fiscal year, payment shall be made by Waste Management 
during the NPP contract period of July 1, 2022 and June 30, 2023. I also understand that 
in exchange for this contribution of $50,000, Waste Management will receive a tax credit 
of $40,000 (an 80% tax credit) from the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.  
 
Waste Management authorizes CALV to submit a proposal to the Pennsylvania 
Department of Community and Economic Development in support of Waste 
Management’s participation in the Slate Belt Rising NPP for this coming fiscal year. 
Waste Management’s commitment is contingent upon the Pennsylvania DCED approval 
of the proposal for the 2022-2023 fiscal year.  
 
We are excited about our participation in the Slate Belt Rising NPP this year and we look 
forward to our partnership with your organization in the revitalization of the Slate Belt. If 
you have any questions, please contact Christopher Farley, cfarley@wm.con.
