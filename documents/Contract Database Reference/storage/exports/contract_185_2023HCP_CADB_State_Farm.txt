Housing Counseling Program (HCP): 
 
At Community Action Lehigh Valley (CALV), it is our duty to make sure that low- to moderate-income families 
interested in homeownership are well equipped to become homebuyers and are well supported in maintaining 
their homes. The Housing Counseling Program, a HUD- and PHFA-approved housing counseling program of 
CALV, offers a number of programs to help Lehigh Valley families achieve homeownership and avoid 
foreclosure. It is HCP’s mission to provide low- to moderate-income individuals and families with a continuum 
of coordinated financial counseling services in one location in order to encourage and assist them to build 
personal assets, to increase and preserve homeownership, and to develop neighborhood wealth for the benefit 
of the whole community. There is no other program in the Lehigh Valley that offers comprehensive 
homeownership counseling, including a first-time homebuyer seminar, pre-purchase and pre-settlement 
counseling, financial education, and foreclosure mitigation and assistance programs comprised of individual 
counseling and a diversion program held in civil court.

HCP’s objectives: Increase homeownership; stabilize neighborhoods and build community wealth, particularly 
for minorities and low- to moderate-income households; and provide foreclosure assistance to homeowners 
facing imminent foreclosure actions. 
 
HCP offers: Home Ownership Counseling Program provides first-time homebuyer educational seminars, pre-
purchase counseling and pre-settlement counseling; Foreclosure Mitigation Counseling Program provides 
counseling services to homeowners in default, at risk of default, or facing foreclosure; and Mortgage 
Foreclosure Diversion Program is a mediation program in the court systems available to homeowners in 
foreclosure. These services increase participants’ self-sufficiency, financial literacy, and asset building by 
providing education, counseling, and assistance regarding homeownership, predatory lending, and mortgage 
foreclosure.  
 
Community Action Development Bethlehem (CADB): 
 
Community Action Development Bethlehem, a subsidiary of Community Action Lehigh Valley, has as its 
mission: “Based on a belief in economic and social justice, CADB improves the quality of life in south 
Bethlehem by fostering economic opportunity, promoting community development, and empowering 
residents to actively participate in the decision-making process regarding the future of our diverse 
community.” The organization is dedicated to working with the residents and business owners of south 
Bethlehem to create sustainable economic development and community revitalization.  
CADB works toward neighborhood stabilization and the economic empowerment of Bethlehem residents, 
through community engagement and promotion of policies, resulting in expanded income, employment, and 
ownership opportunities. CADB also works in partnership with the City of Bethlehem to implement the 
Southside’s Tomorrow 2021-2027 Master Plan, resulting in more economically viable communities.

Evaluation of all CALV programs begins with recordkeeping and data collection, which enables CALV to 
track the progress of program participants during the time they were assisted by the agency and afterward.

defines CALV’s mission, values, strategic assumptions, strategic direction, and five-year strategy for mission 
implementation. As our current five-year Strategic Plan comes to a close, a new plan being developed by the 
CALV Board of Directors and executive staff is nearing completion and will soon come before the full Board 
for approval. In order to ensure that the strategic plan is carried out, the agency develops an Annual Work Plan. 
CALV’s Annual Work Plan consists of measurable goals and performance targets that guide the services and 
activities for every one of CALV’s programs and subsidiaries. The Work Plan of each program or subsidiary is 
evaluated twice yearly in order to measure the effectiveness of services and program outcomes. This evaluation 
is conducted by the Program Development and Evaluation Committee of CALV’s Board of Directors and is 
then approved by CALV’s full Board of Directors.

Departments. CALV’s Fiscal Department is responsible for meeting all contractual fiscal obligations that may 
include submission of time sheets, invoices, and fiscal reports to funders. CALV’s Planning Department and 
Compliance Director provide contract compliance oversight by ensuring that program directors are familiar with 
their contracts, program objectives, and reporting requirements. The Planning Department also conducts periodic 
internal monitoring of all programs that includes reviews of client files, data collection processes, and reports.
