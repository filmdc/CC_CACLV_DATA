PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT 
 
THIS PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made this 18
th
 day of 
May, 2022, by and between ALBERT B. RUTHERFORD c/o COUNTY TAX CLAIM (“Seller”), having 
an address at 669 Washington Street, Easton PA 18042, and COMMUNITY ACTION COMMITTEE OF 
THE LEHIGH VALLEY (“Buyer”), having an address at 1337 East Fifth Street, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania 
18015. 
 
W I T N E S S E T H : 
 
  WHEREAS, Albert B. Rutherford was the owner of record of 124-26 Main St., Glendon 
Northampton County Pennsylvania. The property is more further identified as tax parcel number M9NW3B 
1 9 0813C; and  
 
  WHEREAS the above identified property was exposed to and subject to a Judicial sale for unpaid 
delinquent real estate taxes; and  
 
  WHEREAS, the property was not sold and was placed in the County repository pursuant to 72 
P.S. 5860.626; and 
 
  WHEREAS, the County hereby agrees to sell the property to CACLV pursuant to 72 P.S. 
5860.627; and  
 
  WHEREAS, the County shall issue a deed pursuant to 72 P.S. 5860.627.  
NOW THEREFORE intending to be legally bound and for good and valuable consideration the 
parties hereto agree as follows: 
 
 1.  All WHEREAS Clauses are incorporated into this Agreement by reference.  
 
 2.  Agreement to Sell and Purchase.  Seller agrees to sell to Buyer, and Buyer agrees to 
purchase from Seller, all of Seller’s right, title and interest in and to the following property (collectively, 
the “Property”), upon the terms and conditions contained herein: 
 
(a) All that certain messuage and lot or piece of ground, with the dwelling and any 
other fixtures and improvements situate thereon, under and subject to any appurtenances thereto, located in 
the Borough of Bangor, Northampton County, Pennsylvania, as described by Deed recorded at the Office 
of the Recorder of Deeds in and for Northampton County, Pennsylvania, in Deed Book 723, page 000413, 
having a Northampton County tax parcel identification number of M9NW3B 1 9 0813C and a street address 
of 124-26 Main Street, Glendon PA 18042.

 3.  Purchase Price. The purchase price for the Property (the “Purchase Price”) shall be 
valuable consideration in the amount of Ten Dollars and no cents ($10.00).  
 
 4.  Payment of Purchase Price.  The Purchase Price shall be payable by Buyer as a single 
lump-sum payment to Seller by business check or cash, in Seller’s discretion. 
 
 5.   Deposit.  There shall be no deposit made by Buyer upon the execution of this Agreement. 
 
 6.. Deed Transfer. Seller shall transfer the property to Buyer by Deed, subject to the 
requirements of section 10 of this Agreement, at its earliest convenience once all Seller’s legal requirements 
for repository property sales have been met. 
 
 7. Timetable.  The property shall be transferred to Buyer within six (6) months of the 
completion of the demolition of all improvements onsite.  This timetable may be extended through mutual 
written agreement of Buyer and Seller. 
 
 8. “As Is, Where Is”.  Buyer and Seller agree that any purchase of the Property shall be in 
“as is, where is” condition.  There shall be no implied warranty of habitability of the dwelling, and Seller 
is under no obligation to deliver a clear Certificate of Occupancy.  Seller makes no warranties as to the 
condition of the Property or any improvements, fixtures, or connections existing thereupon.  
 
 9 Title. Pursuant to 72 P.S. 5860.627 Conveyance of the Property shall be conveyed to the 
purchaser, free and clear of all tax and municipal claims, mortgages, liens, and charges and states of 
whatsoever kind, except ground rent separately taxed. 
 
 (a) Seller shall provide good and marketable fee simple title, free and clear of all liens and 
encumbrances not acceptable to Buyer.  Title shall be insurable as such at regular rates by a title insurance 
provider licensed to do business in Pennsylvania.   
 
 (b) Buyer may terminate this Agreement without penalty if Seller is unable or unwilling to 
clear any clouds on the title to Seller’s satisfaction.  Buyer may also elect, but is not obligated, to accept 
the Property with clouds on the title. 
 
 10. Due Diligence.  Buyer and Seller acknowledge that Seller seeks to complete a publicly-
funded construction project that will be in the community development interest of Northampton County.  
Buyer is working with the Northampton County Department of Community and Economic Development 
(“DCED”) to secure public funds.  As such, the deed will not be transferred to Buyer until the following 
studies have been completed and accepted by both Buyer and DCED.  Studies shall be completed by a 
provider of Buyer’s selection. 
 
(a) A Phase I environmental assessment.

 13. No Sales to Third Parties.  During the pendency of this Agreement, Seller may not sell 
or enter into agreement to sell the Property to any third party without the express written permission of 
Buyer.   
 
 14. No Assignment.  Neither Buyer nor Seller may assign their rights under this Agreement to 
any third party without the express written consent of the other party.

 16. Entire Agreement. This Agreement and any approved changes represent the entire

 17. Changes.  No change to any matter of this Agreement shall be binding unless reduced to

 18. Time is of the Essence.  Buyer and Seller agree that time is of the essence with respect to

 22. Severability.  If any portion of this Agreement shall be declared or rendered unenforceable 
or invalid, all remaining sections shall remain in full force and effect.
