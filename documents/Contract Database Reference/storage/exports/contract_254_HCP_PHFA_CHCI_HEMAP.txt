CHAPTER 1.  GENERAL PROGRAM INFORMATION 
I. Introduction 
The Pennsylvania Housing Finance Agency ("PHFA" or the "Agency") has been a long-time supporter of 
housing counseling by providing Pennsylvania residents with direction and support in buying, maintaining, 
and staying in their homes.  PHFA provides housing counseling agencies with the support necessary to 
deliver a full line of professional services to clients.   
In order to maintain the quality of service Pennsylvanians expect from PHFA and the counseling agencies 
it supports, we have established policies in this document to ensure that the services provided are of the 
highest quality and either meet or exceed the standards of our other funding sources. 
By adhering to the protocol contained in this Participation Agreement, the clients can be assured of 
receiving the consistent, high quality counseling services, they have come to expect from PHFA’s 
counseling network. 
II.Intent 
By completing and signing the Participation Certification (Appendix 1), the Housing Counseling Agency 
("HCA") is indicating its intent to be a fully active participant in the Pennsylvania Housing Finance Agency 
Comprehensive Housing Counseling Initiative Network ("Network") and to abide by the requirements, 
policies and standards as set forth in this Agreement, as may be amended from time to time, in addition to 
all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations and guidelines.   
The Comprehensive Housing Counseling Initiative programs consist of counseling for homeowners, 
homebuyers or prospective homebuyers, pre-purchase counseling, debt and default counseling, foreclosure 
prevention counseling, predatory lending counseling, financial planning assistance and counseling in 
connection with PHFA-funded mortgage products.

INTRODUCTION 
The Pennsylvania Housing Finance Agency ("PHFA" or the "Agency") has been a long-time supporter 
of housing counseling by providing Pennsylvania residents with direction and support in buying, 
maintaining, and staying in their homes.  PHFA provides housing counseling agencies ("HCA") with 
the support necessary to deliver a full line of professional services to clients.   
In order to maintain the quality of service Pennsylvanians expect from PHFA and the counseling 
agencies it supports, The Agency has established policies to ensure that the services provided are of 
the highest quality and either meet or exceed the standards of our other funding sources.
Counselors must show proficiency in an array of areas including, but not limited to the home buying 
process, budgeting, money management, credit, banking, mortgage financing, down payment 
assistance, insurance, community involvement, home maintenance, loss mitigation, foreclosure 
prevention, fair housing, delinquency and default intervention, refinancing, anti-fraud or anti-predatory 
lending and relevant federal, state, and local laws. 
Counselors must also exhibit professional communication, effective time management, and 
organizational skills.  These skill sets include the ability to understand, address, assist, guide, and 
suggest solutions to their client’s housing issues. Such requirements are necessary to ensure all 
agencies are adhering to the National Standards for Homeownership Education and Counseling, 
including the Foreclosure Intervention Standards and the Participation Agreement requirements. 
By adhering to the protocol contained in this funding policy, the clients can be assured of consistent, 
high quality counseling services, they have come to expect from PHFA’s counseling agencies. DocuSign Envelope ID: A95E0B88-5C31-4A65-83A5-3E90F0A3F646
