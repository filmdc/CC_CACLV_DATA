It is hard to believe that we are already in November and soon ready to close out the year 2023! We have several priority reminders and updates to share with you as they relate to LIURP and the Customer Outreach department.  Please review this communication carefully and follow up with LIURP team should you have any questions or concerns.  
LIURP Budget and Expectations
Please make sure all your in-progress jobs are inputted in COS by 11/15/23. If you are close to meeting your budget, we advise to cease all LIURP work unless you are confident that you have the available budget. We will be reviewing any unused LIURP funds and will contact agencies to determine if any additional jobs can be completed before 12/31/23. If at any point you know you will not utilize all your 2023 funding, please communicate with UGI ASAP. 
It is important that we work together to achieve the goals and budgeted amounts that have been allotted to your agency.
COS Entry
Remember that the COS should be updated as you complete each step in the LIURP process. You should NOT be holding off on entering everything in the COS until the job is completed. There may be accounts that have intake and/or audits completed that are not going to be weatherized due to deferrals or other reasons. Please make sure to ‘close-no measure’ in the COS ASAP. By failing to enter the information at each step, it prevents the LIURP team from receiving sufficient information on the number of jobs in progress and being pursued by each agency. 
Please remember to enter all necessary measures such as:
Intake and Audit 
Furnace Clean and Tune &amp; Safety Inspection 
Blower Door Tests (pre and post)
Efficiency Test
1st and 2nd Energy Education 
Smoke/CO Alarms
Mileage Reimbursement 
*Please be sure to maximize your $8,000 budget per job so long as your Allotted 2023 Budget Permits*
2024 Contracts
Prior to 2023, we had the ability to carry over any unspent LIURP funds.  Please understand that we do NOT have the option to carry over funds from calendar year 2023 to 2024. The LIURP team and management will be reviewing performance and spending history to determine your 2024 contract and budget allocations. You can anticipate hearing from us by the end of December regarding your proposed budget. Your statement of work and contract details will be sent out by the UGI Procurement department in early 2024 so be on the lookout. 
Waitlist 
We kindly asked all agencies to send your 2024 waitlists to the LIURP team. We also wanted to send a reminder, especially with the abundance of customers who may be placed on a waitlist, that LIURP solicits should be prioritized based on CAP high users and special circumstance customers.  
LIHEAP
The LIHEAP program is officially open! If a customer should contact your agency to inquire about LIHEAP, or you are working with a current LIURP customer, please refer them to our call center or the LIHEAP website to complete an application. We encourage your agency to refer all eligible customers to this program, especially those who have NO HEAT due to an inoperable furnace. LIHEAP Crisis can assist the customer with funding and/or furnace replacements. You should always refer customers who need a furnace replacement to this program before utilizing LIURP funds. This allows more funding to assist the customer with additional energy savings measures. 
A customer can apply for LIHEAP the following ways:
Apply online at  
Visiting their 
Completing a paper application by mail 
*If your agency would like a supply of paper LIHEAP applications to keep on hand, please let us know and we will mail them out to you*

Thank You
Thank you all for another exceptional year. We sincerely appreciate all your hard work and dedication in helping us fulfill our LIURP budgets and meet the needs of our low-income customers. Please continue to communicate with UGI’s LIURP team as it pertains to your budgets, contract goals, and/or any concerns or suggestions that you may have.  We look forward to continued collaboration and shared success in 2024!
