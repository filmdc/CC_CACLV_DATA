Dear Mr. Jennings: 
 
The purpose of this letter is to affirm PPL Electric Utilities (FEIN: 23-0959590) 
commitment to support the Neighborhood Partnership Program of Community 
Action Development Corporation of Bethlehem. I have enclosed a copy of the 
NPP Business/Community Partnership Agreement between PPL Electric Utilities 
and the Community Action Development Corporation of Bethlehem (CADCB), 
which supports PPL’s 6-year commitment of $50,000 to this program.  
 
We are pleased to have the opportunity to contribute $50,000 for fiscal year 
2021, which represents year one of our six-year commitment. Payment shall be 
made by PPL during the NPP contract period of July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022. 
This commitment is subject to CADCB qualifying for the Neighborhood 
Partnership Program of the PA Department of Community and Economic 
Development. I also understand that in exchange for this contribution of $50,000, 
PPL will receive an 80% tax credit from the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.  
 
We look forward to a partnership with your organization in the continued 
revitalization of south Bethlehem.
