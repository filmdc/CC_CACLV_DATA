Second Harvest Food Bank of the Lehigh Valley and Northeast Pennsylvania  
Letter of Intention to Apply for Funding from The Wawa Foundation 
January 25, 2023 
Statement of Need and Summary of Project  
Feeding America estimates that 20% of people served through the charitable food network are 
veterans or have a veteran in their household. Further, according to the most recent data from 
the U.S. Census Bureau’s American Community Survey 5-Year Estimates (2017-2021), there are 
over 59,000 veterans living within Second Harvest’s six-county service territory and 5% are living 
at or below the poverty level, meaning that more than 3,100 veterans are struggling to make 
ends meet, and most likely do not have the funds to purchase nutritious, fresh foods for 
themselves and their families. 
  
According to Feeding America, as many as 24% of active-duty service members and their families 
experience food insecurity.  Service members are often required to move from base to base, 
making it difficult for spouses to maintain consistent work, leaving many families to rely on a 
single income. The family budget is tight when relying on one low-ranking military salary. One-
third of students at Department of Defense schools were eligible for free and reduced-price 
meals during the 2018-2019 school year. 
 
Additionally, though many military families face food insecurity, few qualify for federal food 
assistance programs. When determining SNAP eligibility, housing allowances are considered 
income for service members. According to the USDA, only 2% of active-duty service members 
qualify for SNAP benefits, but about 7% of military members experience food insecurity. 
 
Active military members and veterans are one of the most difficult groups to reach through the 
emergency food network, citing pride as the biggest barrier. To address this need, the USDA, 
Feeding America, and regional food banks have created food distribution programs that break 
down these barriers. Second Harvest’s MilitaryShare program is a part of this nation-wide effort 
and was designed to address the food insecurity needs of military families to promote health and 
financial stability. 
 
The mission of Second Harvest’s MilitaryShare program is “to serve those who served us” by 
providing access to nutritious food, which will assist in improving health outcomes and easing 
financial strain. When funds are tight, often the first thing to be cut from the family budget is 
groceries, especially higher-priced nutritious foods. By supplying struggling military families with 
healthy food, it saves room in the monthly budget for other expenses such as housing, utilities,

and medical bills. By not having to worry about the cost of food, it might mean that families don’t 
have to make the tough choice between paying their rent and keeping food on the table. 
 
Each monthly MilitaryShare contains a box of shelf-stable foods, weighing roughly 20 pounds, 
with additional fresh products such as eggs, milk, meat, and a 10-pound box of produce. Shelf-
stable foods include canned fruits, vegetables, beans and meats, peanut butter, juices, cereals, 
rice, and pasta, with a focus on whole grains and low-sugar products. Each share weighs between 
35-40 pounds.  
 
Second Harvest partners with our food pantry member agencies, local veterans’ groups, county 
veterans’ affairs departments and military and reserve outlets to conduct outreach. Referrals 
from such organizations can make the difference between those in need getting connected with 
our program or remaining unaware. 
 
Currently, Second Harvest distributes MilitaryShares to 480 military families each month across 
six distribution sites, including four pantries within the Second Harvest network, and two 
American Legion sites. Second Harvest pantry sites include: Feeding Families (Monroe), Hamlin 
Food Pantry (Wayne), Mission of Love (Northampton), and Grace Community (Lehigh). American 
Legion sites include Lehighton American Legion (Carbon) and Weatherly American Legion 
(Carbon). 
 
 
Geographic Areas Served 
Second Harvest serves the six Pennsylvania counties of Carbon, Lehigh, Monroe, Northampton, 
Pike and Wayne. Our MilitaryShare program is offered in all six counties. 
 
Project Goals 
Second Harvest operates the MilitaryShare program on an on-going basis. The following 
outcomes will be achieved between July 1, 2022-June 30, 2023:  
• Improve the financial stability of 480 veteran and military households per month by 
providing access to food at six (6) distribution sites. 
• Assist 480 veterans and military households with the maintenance and improvement 
of their physical and mental health by ensuring regular access to nutritious food.

• Engage 100 volunteers for packing and distribution of food boxes. 
• Distribute 200,000 pounds of food. 
 
Requested Amount and Administrative Costs 
Second Harvest respectfully requests $10,000 to support our MilitaryShare program. During the 
last completed program year (July 1, 2021 – June 30, 2022) our administrative costs were 4% of 
total revenue. For the current program year (July 1, 2022 – June 30, 2023) administrative costs 
are budgeted at 6.9% of total revenue.
