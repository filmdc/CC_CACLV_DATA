The purpose of this letter is to affirm Provident Bank’s (FEIN 22-	1211130) commitment to support 
the Neighborhood Partnership Program of Community Action Development Corporation of 
Bethlehem. I have enclosed a copy of the NPP Business/Community Partnership 	Agreement 
between Provident Bank and the Community Action Development Corporation of Bethlehem 
(CADCB), which supports Provident Bank’s 6-year commitment of $50,000 to this program.  
 
We are pleased to have the opportunity to contribute $50,000 for fiscal year 2021, which represents 
year one of our six-year commitment. Payment shall be made by Provident Bank during the NPP 
contract period of July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022. This commitment is subject to CADCB qualifying 
for the Neighborhood Partnership Program of the PA Department of Community and Economic 
Development. I also understand that in exchange for this contribution of $50,000, Provident Bank 
will receive an 80% tax credit from the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. 
 
 
We look forward to a partnership with your organization in the continued revitalization of south 
Bethlehem.
