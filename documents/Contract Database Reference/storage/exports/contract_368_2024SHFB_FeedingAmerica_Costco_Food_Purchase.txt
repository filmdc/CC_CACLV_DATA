Strategic Context
Commitment to Equity 
To achieve our vision of an America where no one is hungry, we are committed to equity, ensuring fair
treatment, access, opportunity, and advancement for all people. We will identify and work towards solutions to
eliminate social, structural, and systemic inequities that contribute to food insecurity, with a focus on
communities that have been historically disadvantaged and/or adversely impacted by racial and/or geographical
inequities. While there are many components to equity, racial inequity has resulted in poorer outcomes for
communities of color, including higher rates of food insecurity and poverty, and worse health outcomes. Please
see our Commitment to Equity for more detail.  
Use of Data to Inform Your Work 
We strongly encourage grantees to utilize national and/or local data to identify and target one or more currently
underserved rural and/or communities of color for this work. The following definitions provide a starting point to
help grantees define and select communities: 
Community - an area identified by the grantee using data (e.g., counties, zip codes, census tracts,
Rural Community is defined as being in county/ies with a USDA Rural Urban Continuum Code (RUCC)
of 4-9. 
Community of Color is defined as predominantly Asian, Black, Latino, Native American, Pacific Islander,
and/or some other race (which the Census defines as any race or ethnicity not included in the preceding
Predominance is typically measured in terms of high proportion or large number, compared to
relevant data point(s) such as averages, medians, differences/disparities, etc. at the national,
regional and/or local levels.
