 Brief Program Description 
 Maximum Word Count = 75 
 
The Sixth Street Shelter provides safe, short-term housing for low-income families with at least one
child under the age of 18 experiencing homelessness. The Shelter provides each family with a fully
furnished apartment and supportive services, including individualized case management. The Sixth
Street Shelter is a vital resource in the community because it is the only shelter in the area where
entire families of any configuration stay together in their own apartments.
