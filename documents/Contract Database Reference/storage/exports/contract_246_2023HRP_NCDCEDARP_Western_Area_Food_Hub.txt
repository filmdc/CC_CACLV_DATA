Western Area Food Hub Project 
Scope of Services 
Background 
The Department of Community & Economic Development (DCED) has been dedicated to 
addressing food insecurity throughout the county. These efforts are part of Northampton 
County’s anti-poverty strategy as outlined in the consolidated plan as well as the annual 
action plan. Having supported food banks in the Slate Belt and Nazareth areas, the western 
portion of the county was identified as an area of need. The existing food bank in 
Northampton Borough is already seen as an established benefit to the community, and has 
room for expanded operation and improvements to serve its community in an even greater 
way that reduces their costs while making their operation more efficient for their 
volunteers. 
Services  
The Scope of Services is to add an additional walk-in freezer to increase the food bank’s 
capacity, as well as additional upgrades to the facility. 
With the expected increase in capacity and coordination with Second Harvest, this project 
will look to make sure that all aspects of the food bank are of a quality that can meet the 
needs of the community and those working at the food bank. The upgrades to the facility 
can include but are not limited to: energy efficient lighting, pallet lift, and energy efficient 
refrigerators.  
Summing up 
 This funding will be used to expand the capacity of the Northampton Area Food 
Bank.   
 Services include the purchase and installment of an additional walk-in freezer and 
other facility enhancements.
