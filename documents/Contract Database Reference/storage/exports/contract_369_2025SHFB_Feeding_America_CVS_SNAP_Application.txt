SNAP Program Overview 
Since 2011, Feeding America has supported network SNAP Application Assistance efforts through member
grants and capacity-building initiatives: creating a strong learning community, identifying best practices, and
accelerating SNAP Application Assistance across the network. In FY24, 174 Feeding America network
members provided SNAP Application Assistance, enabling the submission of more than 345,000 SNAP
applications (more than 292 million meals). 
As the largest federal nutrition program, SNAP is essential to supporting the food security and wellbeing of the
more than 42 million people enrolled.1 However, despite the wealth of evidence that shows SNAP’s positive
impact on improved food security, healthcare costs/indicators, and household financial stability, there remain significant
SNAP participation gaps. Across the country, ~18% of individuals eligible for SNAP are not participating.2 In
addition, the most recent USDA SNAP participation data shows that certain communities that experience food
access barriers are particularly underserved. 
As the largest SNAP application assistor network in the country, the Feeding America network plays an
essential role in ensuring access to SNAP across the nation. Since 2013, Feeding America has awarded food
banks over $23 million in grants to advance SNAP programming. Recipient food banks have used these funds
to invest in the staff, agency training, technology, marketing, and process improvements needed to strengthen
their application assistance activities.  
Strategic Context
As Feeding America works to advance our long-term outcomes and transform the charitable food experience to
ensure that all people always have access to enough food and other resources for an active, healthy life, we
must deepen our insights into the people we serve and the unique barriers to access each community faces.
With the network’s extensive and expanding work in SNAP outreach and application assistance, we have
collectively acquired learnings on the critical role SNAP plays in reducing food insecurity, though it is clear that
additional support can further bolster the impact of SNAP for communities facing hunger.  This grant opportunity
aligns with this direction to increase access to SNAP for communities facing hunger.
Commitment to Equity
To achieve our vision of an America where no one is hungry, we are committed to equity, ensuring fair
treatment, access, opportunity, and advancement for all people. We will identify and work towards solutions to
eliminate social, structural, and systemic inequities that contribute to food insecurity, focusing on communities
that have been historically disadvantaged and adversely impacted by racial and/or geographical inequality.
While there are many components to equity, racial inequity has resulted in poorer outcomes for communities of
color, including higher rates of food insecurity and poverty and worse health outcomes. Please see Feeding
America’s Commitment to Equity page for more detail.

increase in demand for our services.

centers and remote, rural communities. Funding will also be allocated to marketing efforts, in the form of
