Re: PNC Neighborhood Partnership Program Commitment 
 
Dear Mrs. Godshall, 
 
PNC Community Development Banking is pleased to partner with Community Action Development Corporation of 
Allentown (CACDA) as a corporate sponsor for the Third year of a 6 year commitment.  PNC Bank commits to invest 
$50,000 in the Allentown’s Future project, a Neighborhood Partnership Program (NPP) administered by the 
Pennsylvania Department of Community and Economic Development (DCED) under the terms and conditions of 
the Corporate Sponsorship Agreement. 
 
Under the commitment, PNC Bank has pledged to support CADCA with a contribution of $50,000 each year for a 
minimum of six (6) years.  CADCA will apply to the DCED for tax credit authorization that will make the PNC Bank 
contribution eligible for an 80% tax deduction against the company’s Pennsylvania Corporate Taxes. The Federal 
Employer Identification Number for PNC Bank is 22-1146430.  PNC Bank will plan to make the contributions to 
CADCA before December 31, 2021 or once DCED approval is obtained. 
 
We appreciate this opportunity to partner with CADCA.  We look forward to seeing the impact on our shared 
community that will be gained from our collaboration.
