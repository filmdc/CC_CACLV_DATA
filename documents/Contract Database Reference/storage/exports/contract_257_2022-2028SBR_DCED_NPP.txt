Community Action Lehigh Valley respectfully requests an amendment to our Neighborhood Partnership 
Program, Slate Belt Rising, budget for the fiscal year 2022-2023. 
 
To summarize, due to the areas’ school districts improved internet access and support to their students 
following the COVID -19 pandemic, funds in the Broadband Initiative budget were not utilized. Thus, the 
budget item is proposed to be allocated for improving the existing Community Garden in Wind Gap . The 
initiative was started in the FY2016-2022 NPP grant cycle.  
 
In addition, SBR received funding from the Northampton County Hotel Tax , which helped cover some 
promotional expenses so that the marketing and promotion budgets were 	underspent. We also received 
fewer applications from business owners than anticipated for t	he Commercial Lease Subsidy Program, 
and the cost of training events for the SBR Director was less than expected. We propose transferring the 
balances from these budget items to the areas with greater needs - Blight Remediation/Weatherization 
for façade improvement/mural projects and scholarships for high school students.

1) PROJECT TITLE: Marketing and Promotion/Event Funding 
 
Project Description: 
This project is meant to help increase the visibility of the Slate Belt region in our surrounding areas by 
way of print, social, radio and digital media, as well as by hosting a regional event. The objective is to 
encourage visitors to come and see what our small towns have to offer as far as events, dining, and 
entertainment. Currently, our businesses and downtowns have a difficult time attracting visitors from 
other areas and both new and existing businesses struggle to keep their doors open with just local 
traffic. This project will allow us to increase the media footprint of the Slate Belt to reach more people, 
increase foot traffic and continue to use our marketing and branding strategy from the 2016-2017 year 
and to grow our relationship with other local organizations focused on regional marketing and economic 
development, such as Discover Lehigh Valley and Lehigh Valley Economic Development Corporation. 
 
Project Timeline: 
Marketing and promotion will start immediately upon approval of the NPP and will last through the 
summer months and into the fall with all spending to be completed by September 30, 2023. Event 
planning and promotion will take place throu gh Spring 2023, with the event to take place either in the 
Summer or Fall of 2023.
