Partner Network Services Institute Overview
As part of our mission to support agency relations and their agency partner networks across the Feeding
America network, Partner Network Services is pleased to host Agency Partner Capacity Building Foundations
Institute in Chicago, Illinois on October 29-30, 2024. Capacity building within the agency partner network is
critical to increase equitable access to food within the food bank’s service area. The goal of the institute is to
build foundational knowledge that will strengthen relationships and increase agency partner capability.
Conducted by Feeding America’s Partner Network Services team, this institute will bring together up to 15 food
banks and up to 45 agency relations professionals to learn and grow knowledge together. Objectives include
obtaining foundational knowledge about building capacity within the agency partner network, understanding how
to bring an equity lens to increasing food access, and introducing community assessment tools to enable
strategic focus on network engagement and alignment with new Service Area Requirements and Operation
Response Plan requirements in the new Network Agreement.

Who Should Attend: Each food bank should send up to three agency relations team members who support
agency partners in efforts to build capacity to enable them to increase equitable access to food for neighbors.
We strongly recommend sending team members who directly engage with agency partners as a significant part
of their job duties.
This learning opportunity is a foundational course most appropriate for agency relations professionals with up to
2 years of experience or for more tenured staff looking to learn or grow capacity and relationship building skills.
We welcome one agency relations leader/decision maker, but strongly encourage that the majority of attendees
(2 out of 3) work directly with agency partners.
What do we mean by Agency Relations? We use “agency relations” as a blanket term and recognize that
your team may have a different name or varied job titles. Agency Relations staff who have these types of
responsibilities are an ideal match for this training:
Compliance (e.g. food safety, Feeding America and food bank agreement requirements, etc.)
Customer service (e.g. fielding questions from agency partners, coordinating inquiry response with food
Capacity building (e.g. improving agency partner effectiveness and optimization of food distribution)
Plus, other roles as assigned such as agency recruitment, community engagement, Service Insights or
other data analysis, agency enablement food rescue support, government or food bank direct services
programs, etc.
Learning topics will include capacity and relationship building foundations, capacity building during site visits
(before, during and after the site visit/monitoring), finding opportunities for capacity building, introduction into
agency network landscape assessment, neighbor experience, and more.
An optional pre-attendance call will be scheduled to review logistics and other details prior to the institute. The
call will be Wednesday, September 25, 1:00-1:45pm CT. The call will be recorded.  
Grant Period & Amount
Each food bank traveling to the institute will be eligible for $1,500 per person to cover all travel-related expenses
for up to 3 people to attend (max award of $4,500). The institute is being planned in such a way that the $1,500
per person grant should cover most, if not all, travel expenses. A total of $67,500 is available.
The grant period will run from September 1, 2024 – October 31, 2024. We encourage travel arrangements to
be finalized by September 30, 2024.
Selection Process
Your food bank was selected for this opportunity based on your response to the Strategic Capacity
Development Interest Survey in spring 2024.
Participation Requirements
Participants will attend both days of the institute on October 29-30, 2024 at the Feeding America National
Office, 161 N. Clark Street, Chicago, IL 60601.
Each attendee will be expected to fill out a brief pre-institute survey to help provide food bank staff with a better
learning experience. The pre-institute survey is due Thursday, September 12 and will be sent to attendees
identified in this grant application after grant acceptance.  
Food banks will complete an Agency Relations Self-Assessment, if not already completed in 2024.
