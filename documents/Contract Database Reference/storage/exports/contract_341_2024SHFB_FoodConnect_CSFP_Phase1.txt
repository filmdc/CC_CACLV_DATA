Purpose: <p>This Service Agreement establishes the relationship between SHFB and FoodConnect, a nonprofit organization focused on technology and logistics services in relation to hunger relief. This is a trial partnership, limited to Lehigh and Northampton County CSFP delivery, with the potential for expansion. This agreement is funded through a <a href="http://contracts.caclv.org/contracts/306">Feeding PA grant</a>.</p><p>FoodConnect will be providing delivery services for CSFP boxes in Lehigh and Northampton Counties. Boxes will be distributed directly to seniors' homes. FoodConnect will provide detailed distribution reports, for use in reporting back to Feeding PA.</p><p>The date of this agreement runs from March 1, 2024 - June 30, 2024.</p><p>FoodConnect will bill monthly on actual delivery volumes, according to the below payment schedule.</p><p>Questions regarding this service agreement should be directed to Kate Colyer, Regional Program Director, Northeast at <a href="kate@foodconnectgroup.com">kate@foodconnectgroup.com</a>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="http://10.55.0.10/storage/941/image.png"></figure><p>&nbsp;</p>
