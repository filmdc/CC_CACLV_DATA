PROPOSAL TO THE MARTIN GUITAR CHARITABLE FOUNDATION – FY23-24 
 
Overview Of Organization: Community Action Development Bethlehem 
 
Community Action Development Bethlehem (CADB), a subsidiary of Community Action Lehigh 
Valley, is located in south Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, and was incorporated as a 501(c)(3) 
organization on October 6, 1997. The organization is dedicated to working with the residents and 
business owners of south Bethlehem to create sustainable economic development and community 
revitalization. CADB’s mission states: “based on a belief in economic and social justice, CADB 
improves the quality of life in south Bethlehem by fostering economic opportunity, promoting 
community development, and empowering residents to actively participate in the decision-making 
process regarding the future of our diverse community.” 
 
The history of Community Action Development Bethlehem starts with its parent organization 
Community Action Lehigh Valley. The organization started in 1964, with the formation of a 
steering committee of the first Economic Action Program with an annual budget of $34,535 in 
Bethlehem. On December 20, 1965, when Sargent Shriver led the Office of Economic Opportunity 
and launched the nationwide Community Action Program, Community Action Lehigh Valley was 
officially incorporated as a 501(c)(3) non-profit corporation. Today, Community Action Lehigh 
Valley has an operating budget of $20,648,330 and houses four subsidiaries including Community 
Action Development Bethlehem. 
 
Request For Program Support 
 
Funds requested from the Martin Guitar Charitable Foundation will be utilized by Community 
Action Development Bethlehem (CADB) to assist residents and business owners of south 
Bethlehem to create sustainable economic development and community revitalization. CADB 
carries out its mission through community organizing activities and services that foster community 
ownership. Community ownership includes the creation and development of businesses and the 
political and problem-solving process around issues that affect the community. Leadership is 
provided by the Board of Directors of CADB, which is comprised of residents, small business 
owners, and area civic leaders interested in improving the quality of life in south Bethlehem. Staff 
work diligently to develop relationships among business owners and residents of south Bethlehem. 
They also learn about neighborhood needs from the residents, identify residents interested in taking 
an active role in ownership of their neighborhoods, develop neighborhood leaders, and organize 
residents to use their collective power to solve neighborhood problems. The goal for CADB staff 
is to empower residents to have a greater voice in their community while encouraging and assisting 
them to attain more economic independence. 
 
Through Southside’s Tomorrow Neighborhood Partnership Program, Community Action 
Development Bethlehem carries out several projects that are created by and for the residents of 
south Bethlehem. These projects align with the focus areas outlined in the Southside's Tomorrow 
Master Plan: Public and Green Spaces, Affordable Housing, and Communication and 
Connectivity.

Projects include community building events, community gardening, youth programming and 
facade improvements to name a few. In addition, CADB's office acts as a hub for information and 
referral services connecting residents to resources provided by local organizations. 
 
We appreciate the previous support that we have received from the Martin Guitar Charitable 
Foundation and would love to continue our valuable partnership here in south Bethlehem. We 
respectfully request $10,000 in support of CADB and its programming on the Southside. 
 
Budget Overview For Community Action Development Bethlehem 
 
For the 23-24 fiscal year, Community Action Development Bethlehem has a budget of 697,577. 
About half of the annual budget is provided by the Neighborhood Partnership Program (NPP) of 
the Pennsylvania Department of Community and Economic Development (PA DCED).  We are 
proud to have had the PA DCED approve our NPP, Southside’s Tomorrow, in November 2022 for 
the 2023-2024 fiscal year.  This will be the third year of our six-year partnership. Each year, CADB 
will administer the program by allocating $325,000 yearly to various projects in community-
identified focus areas of Housing Quality and Affordability, Public and Green Spaces, and 
Communication and Connectivity.
