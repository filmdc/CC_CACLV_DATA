2. ADMINISTRATION OF PROGRAM: The Second Harvest Food Bank of Lehigh Valley 
and Northeast Pennsylvania (Second Harvest) proposes to administer the Emergency 
Food Assistance Program (TEFAP) for Northampton County between October 1, 2021, 
and September 30, 2026. The full amount of the County’s share of federal commodities 
will be distributed to qualified agencies in the County.  
 
Dry and cold storage TEFAP product will be stored at the Second Harvest warehouse 
located at 6969 Silver Crest Rd, Nazareth, PA 18064. Second Harvest will deliver 
allocated product to each participating agency on a previously scheduled day, or the 
participating agency will pick up the product by appointment at the Second Harvest 
warehouse.  Upon removal of the product from the warehouse by the participating agency 
or from Second Harvest’s delivery vehicle, the product becomes the responsibility of the 
participating agency.

ALLOCATION AND DISTRIBUTION: Product will be distributed to participating 
emergency food assistance providers for redistribution to income-eligible households.  
Participating agencies will be required to submit monthly distribution totals to Second 
Harvest. Allocations of product to be distributed to participating agencies will be 
determined by the number of people served at each site. 
 
Second Harvest determined each eligible partner agency’s percentage rate of allocation 
based on the number of individuals served.  The allocation per agency may be reduced or 
increased if the number of partner agencies increases or decreases. In addition, changes in 
the needs of agencies, their responsiveness to requests for reports, and other agency-
related issues may affect allocations.   
 
A list of member agencies currently eligible to receive TEFAP product along with site 
addresses and contact information is attached.  
 
3. ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATION: Second Harvest requires that partner agencies use 
the individual/household income guidelines accepted by the United States Department of 
Agriculture’s (USDA) Emergency Food Assistance Program (TEFAP), currently based 
on 150% of the official poverty rate, in determining eligibility for TEFAP foods.  
Distribution of food will occur according to the normal operating procedures of Second 
Harvest. Partner agencies must verify receipt of food by signing invoices prepared by 
Second Harvest staff.  
 
4. REPORTING: Qualified agencies of Second Harvest participating in the TEFAP are 
required to submit monthly distribution reports to Second Harvest. In turn, Second 
Harvest will tabulate those figures for reporting required by Pennsylvania Department of 
Agriculture. Standard accounting procedures will be used to assure fiscal accountability.  
 
5. NUTRITION AND EDUCATION: Second Harvest will provide recipes, food safety 
information, and other appropriate material to member agencies through a monthly 
Agency Update and other means for distribution to program participants. USDA’s 
MyPlate guidelines will be presented to our partner agencies though our newsletter and 
agency meetings.    
 
6. PUBLICITY:  Second Harvest will spread awareness about the program through written 
newsletters, and by including relevant information on the agency’s website, and social 
media sites. Additionally, Second Harvest will solicit media coverage through the 
submission of press releases and letters to the editor about the program, as appropriate.
