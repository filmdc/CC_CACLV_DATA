The purpose of this letter is to affirm Wells Fargo’s commitment to support the Neighborhood Partnership Program 
of Community Action Development Corporation of Allentown. I have enclosed a copy of the NPP 
Business/Community Partnership Agreement between Wells Fargo and the Community Action Development 
Corporation of Allentown (CADCA), which supports Wells Fargo’s 6-year commitment of $50,000 to this program.  
 
We are pleased to have the opportunity to contribute $50,000 for FY 2022-2023, which represents year three of 
our six-year commitment. Payment shall be made by Wells Fargo during the NPP contract period of July 1, 2022 to 
June 30, 2023. This commitment is subject to CADCA qualifying for the Neighborhood Partnership Program of the 
PA Department of Community and Economic Development. I also understand that in exchange for this contribution 
of $50,000, Wells Fargo will receive an 80% tax credit from the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. The Federal 
Employer Identification Number for Wells Fargo is 94-1347393. 
 
We look forward to a partnership with your organization in the continued revitalization of Allentown.
