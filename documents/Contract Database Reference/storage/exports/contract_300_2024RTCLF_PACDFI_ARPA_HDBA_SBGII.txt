APPENDIX A - USE OF FUNDS 
HDBA SMALL BUSINESS GROWTH PROGRAM II 
 
The new funds granted through Pennsylvania Department of Community and Economic 
Development Contract C000086369 may only be used for providing technical assistance to 
historically disadvantaged small businesses. While not defined by statute or regulation, DCED 
instructed the PA CDFI Network to include that definition in materials submitted to DCED. That 
definition is as follows: 
 
Technical assistance and business counseling services include a variety of services, 
such as business planning, marketing, accounting, finance, and strategy assistance to 
keep businesses viable. CDFIs may also provide support completing financing 
applications and obtaining the necessary business documents to keep their business 
compliant. Many small businesses are unable to secure clients or financing because 
they lack the education, skills, or resources to perform certain management tasks. 
CDFIs can support business owners and their staff with these business function 
supports.
