Congratulations! We are pleased to inform you that your food bank has been selected to receive a FY24 Retail
Product Sourcing Support- New Store Opening Grant thanks to ALDI, Inc. The intent of this grant
opportunity is to support retail product sourcing related to new ALDI stores opening between July and December
2024.
Executive Summary
Overall funding amount to be awarded: $80,000.
Individual grant amount: Partner food banks will receive $1,000 per new ALDI store opening in their
service area, July through December 2024. Total award amounts range between $1,000 - $5,000 each.
Grant period: June 1, 2024 – May 31, 2025
Goal of funding:To Increase agency capacity to improve capability of receiving and distributing retail
product donations.
Confirmation of interest deadline: Thursday, June 20, 2024.
Commitment to Equity 
To achieve our vision of an America where no one is hungry, we are committed to equity (see our 
people. We will identify and work towards solutions to eliminate social, structural, and systemic inequities that
contribute to food insecurity for individuals who have been historically disadvantaged and/or adversely impacted
by racial and/or geographic inequities. 
Donor Overview
ALDI, Inc. is an international retailer originating in Germany with U.S. operations based in Illinois with 1,800
stores in thirty-five states. ALDI, Inc. stores are smaller than typical supermarkets and offer a variety of

perishable, consumable, household, and seasonal items, with more than 90% of the products being ALDI, Inc.

Strategic Context
In 2024, ALDI, Inc. graciously made a $600,000 gift to the Feeding America national office and our Partner Food
Banks in support of retail rescue efforts and the Food Security Equity Impact Fund. As part of this gift, ALDI, Inc.
is committing $80,000 in support of retail product sourcing related to new ALDI stores opening between July and
December 2024.
 
This grant opportunity focuses on enabling food banks and their agencies to address barriers to sourcing and
distributing more retail products to neighbors in need of food assistance. Based on data evaluating agency
enabled members, we believe the most efficient way to grow retail rescue and support equitable food
distribution is through increased pickups at the agency level. The main goal of this grant funding is to build
agency capacity to maximize direct retail pick-up opportunities, with an emphasis on the ability to handle
perishable product in terms of rescuing additional retail poundage. This grant will focus on the investment of
infrastructure/hard assets that support capacity building of agency partners who are interested in conducting
direct retail pick-up at new ALDI, Inc. stores. 
Eligibility Criteria 
ALDI, Inc. is expanding its landscape by opening new stores across the United States. Your eligibility for this
award is based on the proximity of your food bank service area to a new ALDI store(s), opening July through
December 2024.
Grant Period & Award Amount
ALDI, Inc. is providing $80,000 in grant funding to support this opportunity. The grant period will run from June
1, 2024 – May 31, 2025. Grant amounts have been determined by the donor.Partner food banks will receive
$1,000 per new ALDI store opening in their service area, July through December 2024. Grant award amounts
range between $1,000 - $5,000 each.Grant funds must be spent by the end of the grant period.

Reporting Overview
As stated previously, the overall goal of this grant opportunity is to help grow your retail rescue program by
building the capacity of agency partners to improve equitable access to nutritious food. As a result, funding must
directly support building capacity of agency partners to effectively conduct direct retail rescue at new ALDI, Inc.
stores within your service area.
 
Eligible Expenses 
Grant funds may be used to support the following expenses (partially or in full): 
Equipment (new or used) & supplies.
Coolers and freezers
Forklifts, pallet jacks, pallet scales, bins/totes, carts, shelving, etc. 
Food safety equipment/supplies: sanitizer, passive cooling devices- thermal blankets, scales,
thermometers, coolers, agency training materials.
Technology- tablets, smart phones to support agency adoption/use of MealConnect. 
One-time equipment repairs and/or electrical upgrades at agency partner locations.
Ineligible Expenses 
Grant funds may not be used to support the following expenses: 
Expenses at your food bank
Expenses unrelated to building capacity of agency partners to conduct retail rescue at ALDI, Inc. stores.
Purchase/procurement of food
Rent, building maintenance, utility expenses.
Lobbying efforts 
Third-party home delivery services (i.e., DoorDash, Instacart) 
Important Notes 
Food banks may re-grant funds to agency partners that can help execute the grant-funded program. 
Use of the term “agencies” is broad, including current agencies and new partners, but it does NOT
include schools or government partners. Partners should be 501c3 organizations, or federal or state-
recognized Native tribes.
The same restrictions and deadlines for expenditure apply to all sub-grantees. Grantees should
determine the reporting requirements for organizations receiving a sub-award. Note that the food bank
must retain administrative and fiscal responsibility for the sub-grant execution, including timing/use of
funds and reporting.
