We are pleased to inform you that your food bank may opt-in to be considered for a FY23 Employee
Engagement Survey Support grant, thanks to Feeding America. The goal of this grant is to subsidize the
incremental cost for partner food banks to contract with Quantum directly on FY24 employee engagement
surveys, as Feeding America re-evaluates how to best support the network with this initiative moving forward.  
Executive Summary 
Overall funding amount available: Up to $650,000 
Individual award range: $3,350 - $9,100 depending on organization staff size 
Grant period: 7/1/2023 - 6/30/2024 
Goal of funding: Subsidize the incremental cost for partner food bank to contract with Quantum directly on
FY24 employee engagement surveys 
Key considerations: Open to all partner food bank members to apply. If we receive more interest than
funds available, awards will be prioritized based on preferences outlined below.

America will be assessing how best to support members with this initiative moving forward.

way that isn’t disruptive to the network. Through input from the network, we want to make sure that we offer

All grantees are required to use a consistent core set of questions for benchmarking purposes. Additional survey
questions can be added and customized. Since participating members can conduct their annual engagement
survey at a time that is most convenient for their staff, benchmarking will happen dynamically within the
Quantum Workplace dashboard. As surveys are completed the food bank benchmark will dynamically update
for comparative purposes. 
Grantee Selection Process 
All partner food banks are eligible to opt in to this opportunity. If we receive more interest than total funds
available, preference will be given to food banks that: 
Participated in the Quantum employee engagement survey in previous years 
Expressed interest in using Quantum this year via a recent pulse survey  
Staff size less than 150 (if needed)
