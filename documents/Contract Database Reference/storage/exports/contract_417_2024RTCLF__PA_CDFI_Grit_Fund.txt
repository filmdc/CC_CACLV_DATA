PENNSYLVANIA CDFI NETWORK AGREEMENT  
GRIT FUND OPERATIONS GRANT 
 
This Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into between Pennsylvania CDFI Network (the "PA CDFI 
Network” or “Grantor”) and The Rising Tide Community Loan Fund (the "Grantee” or “CDFI”). 
PURPOSE AND BACKGROUND 
The Growth, Resiliency, Independence, Tenacity Fund (GRIT Fund) is a program designed to provide 
resources that support the expansion of Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs) serving 
business owners, particularly those who are People of Color, to increase economic and financial 
opportunities. 
Thanks to generous donations by member banks of the Greater Philadelphia Financial Services 
Leadership Coalition (GPFSLC), the GRIT Fund is expanding its reach beyond Philadelphia to the Lancaster 
and Lehigh Valley regions of Pennsylvania. This expansion will enable the program to support additional 
underserved communities and enhance opportunities for small businesses in these regions, alongside 
the current efforts in Philadelphia. 
The GRIT Fund will support three key components identified by members of the GPFSLC as essential for 
strengthening CDFIs, particularly to ensure they can adequately serve Black and Brown businesses. The 
Pennsylvania CDFI Network coordinates the GRIT Fund and manages contributions from the GPFSLC 
membership. 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing, and subject to the conditions contained herein, the 
parties hereto intending to be legally bound hereby, do covenant and agree for themselves, their 
respective successors and assignees as follows: 
GRANT AMOUNT  
  
Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the PA CDFI Network hereby makes available to the Grantee out 
of funds provided by the Greater Philadelphia Financial Services Leadership Coalition (GPFSLC) a grant 
in the sum of $10,000 subject to the condition that it shall be used by the Grantee to carry out the 
activities described in this Agreement and Appendix A. This grant shall be disbursed to the CDFI with a 
single lump sum payment. 
  
The amount of this Agreement may be modified by future amendment to this Agreement as warranted 
based on additional revenue received from GPFSLC. Any future payments after the initial will be 
contingent upon the receipt of funds from GPFSLC, timely reporting and performance of the CDFI in 
completing the Agreement requirements.

AGREEMENT REQUIREMENTS  
  
The following conditions are imposed for the Grantee to receive funds under this Agreement for the 
GRIT Fund Operations Grant. The Grantee must maintain good standing as a member of the PA CDFI 
Network, including annual dues payments, and agree to support fund raising activities that allow growth 
and expansion of the PA CDFI Network. 
 
Violation of this agreement may result in action(s) that reduce or eliminate the grant funding provided 
to the CDFI and/or remove the CDFI from membership in the Network. 
 
The Grantee responsibilities for this funding and the GRIT Fund (“the Program”) are outlined below. 
 
 
 GRANTEE RESPONSIBILITIES 
  
Grantees must fulfill obligations for the following to receive the funds granted in this Agreement:  
  
1. Attend PA CDFI Network meetings and Program training for staff.  
 
2. Assist in activities that support the administration of the Program by the PA CDFI Network.  
 
3. Outreach to small businesses and organizations or associations composed of businesses to 
provide financing for small business operational and expansion needs. 
 
4. Provide technical assistance to applicants for the Program that seek to apply through the Grantee 
and assist those businesses to complete applications submitted for financing through GRIT Fund 
capital investment.  That assistance includes, but is not limited to business counseling, training 
and guidance to help businesses perform management tasks, and connecting these businesses 
to appropriate professional services.
