*Detailed Grant Description
The mission of Community Action Lehigh Valley is to improve the quality of life in the Lehigh Valley by building a community in which all people have
access to economic opportunity, the ability to pursue that opportunity, and a voice in the decisions that affect their lives.
Community Action Lehigh Valley's programs work to combat poverty in neighborhoods with significant demonstrated need. In the Lehigh Valley
metropolitan area, the unemployment rate is 5.6%, and the poverty rate is 10.4%. These figures are significantly higher in the densely populated cities
such as Allentown and Bethlehem. In Allentown, the unemplo yment rate is 9.6% and the poverty rate is 23.3%. In Bethlehem, the unemployment rate is
5.6% and the poverty rate is 16.8%.
These economic struggles are compounded b y an extremely inflated housing market. Since the massive, nationwide shifts in the housing market resultant
from the COVID-19 pandemic, the Lehigh valley has seen both home prices and rental prices rise at an unprecedented rate. According to a housing data
report published by RentCafe, the Lehigh Valley was the second most competitive small rental market in the United States. In terms of home sales, the
latest numbers from the Greater Lehigh Valley Realtors show that the median sales price was up to $300,000, a year-over-year increase of 15.6%. There
remains a critical shortage of affordable housing in the Lehigh Valley, and the issue will persist until addressed by a dedicated coalition of local
governments, housing developers, and non-profit community organizations.
The Bank of America Foundation's generous contribution will enable these three programs to continue assisting local residents in obtaining and
maintaining affordable, safe housing through the Community Action Homes initiative and the Housing Counseling Program, as well as supporting existing
and prospective entrepreneurs through the Rising Tide Community Loan Fund. 
Additional Information
The mission of the Rising Tide Community Loan Fund, a federally-certified Community Development Financial Institution (CDFI) is to identify small
business and community development credit needs that stifle the creation of economic opportunity in low- to moderate-income communities of the Lehigh
Valley and to meet those needs with affordable credit products.
The mission of the Housing Counseling Program is to provide low-to moderate-income families with a continuum of coordinated services in one location to
better meet their housing and consumer credit needs and to build individual and community wealth.
Community Action Homes is a comprehensive housing revitalization initiative that focuses resources in targeted areas of Allentown, Bethlehem,
Northampton County, and Lehigh County to undertake: acquisition/rehab/resale; owner-occupied rehab; investor-owned rehab, and owner-occupied,
investor-owned and commercial facades. These impro vements aim to enhance the health, well-being, and quality of life of residents, increase property
values, and create wealth for the residents and revenue for the cities and counties.

*Evaluating and Measuring Impact
Community Action Lehigh Valley's activities are driven by a board-approved strategic plan that defines Community Action Lehigh Valley 's mission, values,
strategic assumptions, strategic direction, and five-year strategy for mission implementation. To ensure that Community Action Lehigh Valley's strategic
plan is effectively carried out, the agency develops an annual work plan that consists of measurable goals and performance targets that guide the services
and activities for every one of Community Action Lehigh Valley's programs and subsidiaries. The annual work plan is evaluated twice a year by the
agency's Board of Directors to measure the effectiveness of services and program outcomes. Communit y Action Lehigh Valley staff documents services
provided using comprehensive client files and ClientTrack software. In addition, some programs utilize program-specific databases. Community Action
Lehigh Valley's small business lending arm uses a specialized database to monitor and track lending activity and housing counselors use Rx Office.
Success is also measured through contract compliance activities and monitoring conducted by Community Action Lehigh Valley's planning and fiscal
departments. Community Action Lehigh Valley's program participants are also given the opportunity to evaluate the program to help Community Action
Lehigh Valley to evaluate the effectiveness of programs. For example, those who participate in the Home Ownership Counseling seminars are ask ed to
complete a survey. This data is analyzed and reviewed.
