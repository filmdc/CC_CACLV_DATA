FY2023-2024 Pennsylvania Agricultural Surplus System (PASS) 
Memorandum of Understanding 
Between Feeding Pennsylvania as PASS Supplier and 
_Second Harvest Food Bank of the Lehigh Valley and Northeast Pennsylvania as PASS Subcontractor 
Commitments by the undersigned PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor: 
 
1. PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor will conduct all PASS activities in accordance with the PASS Act, 
PASS guidelines established by the PA Department of Agriculture (PDA), and all applicable federal, state, 
and local regulations. 
2. To be eligible for PASS, food must be grown or produced in Pennsylvania, must be eligible under the State 
Food Purchase Program (SFPP).  Wholesale or retail food products are not eligible. 
3. PASS will be conducted in a manner that provides enhanced market opportunities for eligible growers and 
producers throughout the Commonwealth, and for a wide variety of Pennsylvania agricultural products.  
Accordingly, PASS Subcontractor will use best efforts to source PASS-eligible product from each of their 
assigned counties in order to expand the list of growers and producers. 
4. In conducting PASS activities, PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor will leverage their extensive 
capacities and experience in handling/distributing emergency food under the SFPP, The Emergency Food 
Assistance Program, or the Feeding America system, and qualify eligible Consumers consistent with 
existing practices for these programs. 
5. PASS Subcontractor will provide to the PASS Supplier an annual plan of their purchasing for the 
forthcoming Fiscal Year by July 21, 2023. PASS Subcontractors will also be required to have a 
representative of their organization on a bi-weekly conference call led by Feeding Pennsylvania’s Director 
of Agricultural Partnerships to provide an update on their food sourcing and distribution using PASS funds. 
6. PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor will execute all purchases and commitments for food and 
associated supplies, as evidenced by PASS-compliant invoices, by June 30, 2024.  Complete and accurate 
documentation of these purchases and commitments must be provided to PASS Supplier by July 10
th
, 2024.  
If necessary, PASS Supplier will spend uncommitted funds on your behalf. 
7. PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor will complete all services by August 15, 2024, to include food 
delivered by PASS Supplier to PASS Subcontractor, food distributed by PASS Subcontractor to their 
Member Agencies, and freight and other services needed to distribute food product. 
8. PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor will complete all PASS-related payments by August 15, 2024. 
9. PASS Supplier will ensure that PASS Funding is allocated for all 67 Pennsylvania counties in accordance 
with the approved SFPP Allocation ratios for FY 2023-2024.  It is the responsibility of the PASS 
Subcontractor to carry out these allocations and distribute product at a value as close as possible to 
the dollar values allocated to their assigned counties. 
a. Assigned Counties: Carbon, Lehigh, Monroe, Northampton, Pike, Wayne 
10. PASS Supplier will provide to the PASS Subcontractor their respective funding allocation information 
within 10 business days of receiving the fully executed Purchase Order and SFPP Allocation schedule from 
PDA. 
11. PASS Supplier has the capability to acquire and deliver to all PASS Subcontractors all required PASS 
product, but will offer to the PASS Subcontractor the option of retaining all or a portion of the food and 
associated funding to facilitate local acquisition and to honor existing and new agriculture relationships. 
a. USDA Farm to Food Bank – This federally funded program will require a match from the state in 
the form of PASS funds.  These federal funds cannot be used to purchase food and must be used 
for packaging, harvesting, transporting, or processing products.  Clear and accurate, itemized 
invoices will be required. Expenditure and distribution reporting consistent with general PASS 
funding will be required. PASS Supplier will work with PASS Subcontractor and their vendors to 
coordinate eligible purchases and paperwork to assign toward this grant. 
12. PASS Supplier and PASS Subcontractor will not charge shared maintenance, purchase mark-ups, or any 
other add-on fees for PASS product.  Funding has been allocated within the PASS Purchase Order to pay 
reasonable freight and other supply costs (to acquire and transport product to the PASS Subcontractor’s 
facility) and to compensate for PASS Subcontractor distribution to Member Agencies in the form of

administrative fees.  The amount of this funding is based on the formula derived from the SFPP Allocation 
schedule, as well as the PASS Subcontractor achieving all reporting and performance objectives outlined in 
this agreement. 
13. PASS Supplier, PASS Subcontractor, and Member Agencies will not charge money or require service from 
Consumers to as a condition of receiving PASS food. 
14. To enable the PASS Supplier to meet reporting requirements to PDA, PASS Subcontractor will document 
and report to PASS Supplier detailed sourcing and distribution information: 
a. source of product (vendor name, street address, county, and city of origin) 
b. product types and unit cost 
c. pounds of food distributed 
d. the street address and manner of each distribution 
e. the number of households served and method of determining households served 
15. To assist in managing the PASS program, PASS Subcontractor will be required to supply reports including 
expense/reimbursement reports in Microsoft Excel format by email to PASS Supplier 
(tmainzer@feedingpa.org) by the 10
th
 calendar day after each month until program completion.  Invoicing 
and delivery confirmation paperwork will also be required. The PASS Supplier will in turn be submitting 
monthly reports to PDA. 
16. To aid in advocacy efforts, PASS Subcontractor is required to obtain written testimonials and other 
feedback about the PASS program from Member Agencies, clients, and agriculture partners. 
17. PASS Subcontractor is required to distribute no less than 50% of their total funding allocation no later than 
December 31, 2023.  If 50% of funding is not distributed by 12/31/23, an updated workplan must be 
provided to PASS Supplier by 1/31/24 detailing how funds will be spent in final six months of program 
year.   Additionally, PASS Subcontractor is also required to distribute PASS-funded products in each of 
their respective counties no later than December 31, 2023.   
18. Failure by the PASS Subcontractor to adhere to the terms of this agreement may result in PASS Supplier 
withdrawing some or all of a PASS Subcontractor’s remaining funding in order to meet PASS Supplier’s 
responsibilities as statewide contractor.  Any reallocation or withdrawal of funding will be communicated 
to PASS Subcontractor in advance; amounts and timing of reallocations will be determined at the sole 
discretion of PASS Supplier based on available information.  It is the responsibility of the PASS 
Subcontractor to maintain accurate financial records of all PASS activity and report that activity to PASS 
Supplier in a timely manner. 
19. Any PASS Subcontractor that fails to comply with all PASS statutory and program requirements may result 
in, at the discretion of the PASS Supplier, loss of contract.  PASS Supplier reserves the right to suspend or 
disqualify any PASS Subcontractor for demonstrated noncompliance. 
20. No Current or Prior Conflict of Interest: That Subcontractor has no business, professional, personal, or 
other interest or relationships, that would conflict in any manner or degree with the performance of its 
obligations under this Agreement. 
21. Notice of Potential Conflict: If any such actual or potential conflict of interest arises under this Agreement, 
Subcontractor shall immediately inform the PASS Contractor in writing of such conflict. 
22. Remedy for Material Conflict: The PASS Contractor will make a determination of actions in dealing with 
such conflict(s) of interest.  If such conflict poses a material conflict to and with the performance of 
Subcontractor's obligations under this Agreement, then the PASS Contractor may choose to terminate the 
Agreement immediately upon written notice to Subcontractor; such termination of the Agreement shall be 
effective upon the receipt of such notice by Subcontractor.
