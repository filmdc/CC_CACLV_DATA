Please provide a summary of how you plan to utilize grant funds. Include anticipated cost categories
and estimated amounts.
We plan to utilize grant funds to support our retail recovery program. Retail recovery connects grocery
stores directly with partner agencies, resulting in less food waste and more healthy food going to people
in need. We currently partner with 60 retail stores, with 40 pantries participating. In 2022, we rescued
2,228,000 pounds of food.
Our Retail Recovery Coordinator oversees the program, onboards new retail partners, provides education
and support to existing retail partners, supports food pantry partners, and serves as the link between the
store and pantry.
CVS General Operating funds will be used to support the salary and benefits of our Retail Recovery
Coordinator at $21,210.19 per year for two years, totaling $42,420.38.
