Re:	Reprogramming of 2017 PHARE grant
Community Action’s Housing Counseling Program (HCP), formerly known as Community Action Financial Services, received an $83,000 2017 PHARE grant to operate a Home Ownership Savings Account Program and to provide other pre-purchase counseling services.
The program has involved participants saving funds for a home purchase in the amount of $2,000 to earn a $2,000 savings match funded through the PHARE grant.  While the program has been a success in terms of promoting savings habits, not enough participants went on to purchase a home.  This has hindered the use of grant funds.
HCP seeks to ensure this grant can be fully expended by September 30, 2024.  As of the last semi-annual report submitted, $50,361 remained as of June 30, 2023.  Toward the goal of timely spending, HCP respectfully requests PHFA’s permission to use these funds for either the original Home Ownership Savings Account Program and related pre-purchase services or for owner-occupied housing rehabilitation activities.HCP’s sister program, Community Action Homes, operates owner-occupied housing rehab programs throughout Lehigh and Northampton County.  This program provides health, safety, and code compliance improvements to low-income homeowners’ residences.  Community Action Homes has a waiting list of over 400 interested participants, so it would not be difficult to reprogram the funds to owner-occupied rehabilitation.  Projects are typically capped at $24,999 to avoid the need for prevailing wage.  
Community Action Homes proposes to immediately complete one (1) job, with another job completed by September 30, 2024.  Community Action Homes income-certifies all participating households.  Owner-occupied rehab funded through this grant will prioritize households with incomes below $34,450, and in no cases will serve households with incomes above $55,120, to honor the terms of the original grant.
Please note HCP is unable to determine the exact allocation of funds between the Home Ownership Savings Account Program and owner-occupied rehab at the moment.  There are currently three active savers in the Home Ownership Savings Account Program, that could potentially earn the $2,000 savings match if they purchase a home.  HCP estimates spending $39,000 of the remaining grant funds on owner-occupied rehab, but this is subject to change.
If you have any questions on the original PHARE grant, activities to date under this grant, or the Home Ownership Savings Account Program, please do not hesitate to contact HCP Director Susan Gottwald (; 484-893-1036).  If you have any questions on Community Action Homes’ owner-occupied rehab activities, please do not hesitate to contact me (; 484-893-1061).
Thank you for your consideration of this request, and for Pennsylvania Housing Finance Agency’s continued support of Community Action Lehigh Valley through the PHARE program.  This funding source, and PHFA’s partnership, is greatly beneficial to our agency.
