 Project Description
 
Our agricultural incubator provides access to land, equipment, education, and infrastructure to
address the barriers of new farm entry. We emphasize access for Black, Indigenous, People of
Color, and women, as well as diversified farming techniques that allow beginning famers to test
their business models and crop plans. Our goal is to help new farmers create viable careers to
address the concerning decline in farmers across our region and the nation. '
The Seed Farm is a dynamic urban farming program on 42 acres that increases access to
agriculture at all levels, from the farmers in our incubator program to families facing food
insecurity. We are working toward creating a stronger local food economy, in which everyone
has access to the foods needed to keep themselves and their families healthy. In 2023 we are
introducing our Growing Partners community garden program, the goal of which is to increase
access to nutritious, fresh foods for residents in low-income environments and to provide
opportunities so they can grow their own food and gain agency over their lives. Second Harvest
pantries and community organizations will have the opportunity to request the varieties of
vegetables and herbs they would like to grow. Partners will receive the materials for raised beds,
supplies, soil, plants, and support from The Seed Farm staff. '
Upgrades on-site at the farm, coupled with the establishment of community gardens, and
support for our Farm Manager, will have a tremendous impact on our work to build a thriving
and equitable local food economy.'

 Mission Statement 
 
The Seed Farm is a program of Second Harvest Food Bank of the Lehigh Valley and Northeast PA.
The mission of Second Harvest, is “to obtain food and distribute it to people in need through area
non-profits, and to provide resources for education and advocacy to end hunger.” Second Harvest
provides nutritious food, offers nutrition education, advocates to end food insecurity, dismantles
barriers to create access, and works to attain food justice. Second Harvest respects and preserves
the dignity of each of our neighbors.
The Seed Farm carries out a critical part of this mission. There can be no access to nourishing food
without farms. The Seed Farm’s primary goal is to support the creation of new agricultural
businesses and to support the local food economy, including the charitable food network, with an
emphasis on getting nutritious and locally-grow food to under-resourced communities.
The Seed Farm is part of a broader anti-poverty organization through its affiliation with Second
Harvest, which is a program of Community Action Lehigh Valley (CALV). The mission of CALV is “to
improve the quality of life in the Lehigh Valley by building a community in which all people have
access to economic opportunity, the ability to pursue that opportunity, and a voice in the decisions
that affect their lives.”
