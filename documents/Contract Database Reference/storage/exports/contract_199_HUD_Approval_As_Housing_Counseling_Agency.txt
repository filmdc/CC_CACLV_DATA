I.        PURPOSE: 
 
The purpose of this review is to evaluate the housing counseling agency’s ability to 
ensure compliance with HUD Housing Counseling Program requirements, including 
24 CFR, Part 214, HUD Housing Counseling Handbook 7610.1, applicable mortgagee 
letters, other guidance, and grant agreements if applicable.  Additionally, this review is 
conducted to ensure that the housing counseling agency is implementing its programs and 
services in accordance with the counseling work plan submitted to HUD. 
 
II.      METHOD AND SCOPE OF REVIEW : 
 
          This review entailed Beth Eilers interviewing staff members of Community Action of 
Lehigh Valley, Inc., including Susan Gottwald, Director; Annette Santiago, Certified 
Housing Counselor; Julie Shoults, Program Planner/Grant Writer; Debra Kiefer, Billing; 
Katherine Duque, Staff Member, and Demier Casteel, PHFA Representative. 
  
           The files below were reviewed for compliance with the Notice of Funding Availability   
           (NOFA) Applications, Grant Agreement, Federal Register and OMB requirements and   
            Handbook and Regulation requirements. The file types reviewed included -   
           10 - Delinquency/Default, 2 – First Time Homebuyer Seminars, and 1 – Financial 
Education Course. 
 
            The following files were reviewed:  
          
              Client File Numbers: 2814400, 2811909, 2808388, 2808274, 2473036, 2472119,  
               2469664, 1579602, 2331968, 233091 
 
               Group Education: 1/8/22 & 1/15/22(FTHB) and 6/14/22, 6/16/22, 
               6/21/22 & 6/23/22(Financial Education Course) 
         
            
            The items reviewed included the following: 
 
• Financial, budget and/or credit analysis documents 
• Activity Logs 
• Action Plans 
• Follow-up communication notes 
• Documented results of counseling 
• Disclosure statement provided to client 
• Termination of counseling statement with date counseling was ended

• Funding source(s) and amounts to which counseling activities are attributed, if 
applicable 
• Authorization to obtain credit report 
• Documentation of client fees paid, and fee schedule disclosure provided to client 
 
 
III.      GENERAL COMMENTS : 
 
Community Action Committee of Lehigh Valley, Inc.(CACLV), an LHCA under parent 
agency PHFA, has been an approved HUD Housing Counseling Agency since 2003. In the 
last grant cycle, CACLV received a subgrant in the amount of $29,160 from parent agency 
PHFA. 
 
CACLV provides counseling service in the Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton, PA metro area. 
 
The majority of CACLV’s one-on-one clients are receiving Delinquency 
Default/Foreclosure Prevention counseling, followed by Pre-purchase and Rental clients.  
The topics covered by their group education sessions are First Time Homebuyer seminars 
and Financial Literacy. Both individual and group education counseling is offered in 
person and online. 
 
            
IV.      OBSERVATIONS: 
 
            When reviewing this agency’s audited financial statements dated June 30, 2021, findings  
             were discovered regarding a material weakness in managing federal award funds. These  
             awards were not housing counseling funds, but still a cause for concern.  
               
V.       FINDINGS: 
 
            None

 The Office of Housing Counseling, upon the satisfactory completion of their FY 2023
