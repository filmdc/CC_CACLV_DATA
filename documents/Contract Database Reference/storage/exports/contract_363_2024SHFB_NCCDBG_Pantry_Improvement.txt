Second Harvest Food Bank Infrastructure Upgrades  
Northampton County CDBG Program  
  
Scope of Work  
The need in Northampton County is great. According to Feeding America’s Map the Meal Gap 2022, the 
food insecurity rate for Northampton County is 10.7% (33,750 people). The rate is higher for children, at 
13.9% (8,490 children). Populations facing food insecurity are strongly correlated with those that have 
been impacted by systemic racism and economic exploitation. The food insecurity rate across 
Northampton County is higher for Black (15%) and Hispanic (21%) individuals, as compared to their white 
counterparts (11%).  
The Second Harvest network continues to serve historic volumes of people. In FY 2023-2024, our 
Northampton County pantries served an average of 24,150 people each month. Second Harvest is 
committed to meeting the food access needs of the residents of Northampton County, with an emphasis 
on fresh and nutritious foods.  Our increased acquisition, storage, and distribution of additional food to 
meet the rising needs of our community, has caused stress on our warehouse facility.  
Second Harvest will utilize $25,000 of Northampton County CDBG funding to support infrastructure 
upgrades to our food bank facility, located at 6969 Silver Crest Road, Nazareth, PA 18064. The necessary 
upgrades will ensure the safety of the food we provide to the community, as well as the safety of the food 
bank team that distributes the food.
