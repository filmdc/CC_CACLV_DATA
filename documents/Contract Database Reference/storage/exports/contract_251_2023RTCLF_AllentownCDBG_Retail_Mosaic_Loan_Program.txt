City of Allentown 
Retail Mosaic Self-Forgiving Loan Program  
 
Pre-Cursor –  To be considered for participation in the program, an applicant must agree to 
hire one low-moderate income full time equivalent (FTE) job; hiring from within 
the City-wide area, (as per Allentown Five Year Consolidated Plan 2019-2024) 
(Demonstrate in application)- 22 points 
 
Eligible Businesses Retail, Mercantile, Service, Commercial (Restaurants and other establishments 
that prepare made to order food are ineligible) 
Businesses must demonstrate a positive, direct community impact and choose 
to open within the City-wide area (as per Allentown Five Year Consolidated Plan 
2019-2024). Businesses must agree to create one full time equivalent job which 
can be held by individuals residing within the City-wide area who meet the 
low/moderate income guidelines found on page 4. 
Entrepreneurs interested in establishing retail businesses within city limits.

All applications will be pre-screened for completeness and adherence to the 
above by City Staff.   Applications will be forwarded to Rising for underwriting 
and recommendation.  Rising Tide will solicit credit reports on all prospective 
borrowers. 
Rising Tide will administer the credit underwriting of the Retail Mosaic Self-
forgiving Loan Program in adherence to CDBG program guidelines.  Rising Tide 
will underwrite referred applications and will submit their recommendation to 
City staff.  The City Staff person will forward these recommendations to a Retail 
Mosaic Award Committee.  The Retail Mosaic Award Committee will review the 
recommendations and awards will be made to prospects based on fit/need for 
services and uniqueness to the city wide business landscape.  All awards are 
contingent upon final approval and underwriting by Rising Tide.  
 
Loan Closing Rising Tide will prepare all loan documents and perform loan closings, in 
concurrence with CDBG’s job creation requirements.  A clawback will exist in the 
loan documentation that requires payment of loan in full for any recipient that 
moves their business within five years, does not meet the job creation 
requirements, or closes the business. 
  
Eligible Use of funds Acquisition and installation of new or used machinery, equipment which is 
directly related to the business process.  Upgrading existing machinery and 
equipment which is directly related to the business process is also eligible. 
 	Successful award recipients must obtain one to three written quotes for 
equipment purchases.  If only one quote is obtained, the award recipient shall 
provide reasonable documentation justifying why only one quote could be 
obtained.  
 	This loan program is a reimbursement program.  Applicants will need to utilize 
bridge or other financing to acquire equipment prior to the distribution of loan 
proceeds. 
 	Please note:  Repair costs for existing machinery and equipment, labor costs and 
refinancing and related party transactions are not eligible for loan 
reimbursement. 
 
Job Monitoring Rising Tide will annually monitor all companies for meeting program 
requirements.  City of Allentown HUD Office will monitor Rising Tide annually.
