Community Action Lehigh Valley is seeking funds to both modernize and provide maintenance and

tie factory, in thanks to the generosity of Al and Betty Fantozzi and hundreds of donors, including the 
Helen & R.K. Laros Foundation in the amount of $100,000. Today, the Forte building houses 
Community Action Lehigh Valley’s administrative offices and several on-site programs. In addition, the 
Forte building houses 10 apartments that participate in the Housing Choice Voucher Program, formerly 
known as Section 8. These apartments are owned and operated by Valley Housing Development 
Corporation, in partnership with Community Action Lehigh Valley.'
