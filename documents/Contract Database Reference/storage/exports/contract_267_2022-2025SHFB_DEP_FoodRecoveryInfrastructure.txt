SCOPE OF WORK The grantee will purchase food recovery equipment for the proper management and 
operation of food waste reduction and recovery.  The only equipment eligible for funding include refrigerant or 
non-refrigerant vehicles, box trucks, cargo vans, freezers, refrigerators, stoves, ovens, microwaves, dollies, hand 
lift trucks, pallet jacks or equivalent.  Also eligible for funding under this grant agreement is the installation of 
the approved project equipment.  Installation is defined under this agreement as shipping, delivery, placement, 
assembly and set-up only. The equipment must be utilized by a Pennsylvania registered nonprofit organization 
to repurpose and redistribute apparently safe and wholesome food segments to the general public.  Any food 
material not utilized in the project must be composted to the greatest extent possible.  
 
Costs not eligible for funding under this grant agreement include but are not limited to passenger vehicles 
(including pick-up trucks, cars, SUV’s, mini-vans), labor, warranties, insurance, tax and tags, 
installation/upgrades (electrical/plumbing/demolition), site improvement and preparation, construction or 
building costs, tables, shelving, pans, utensils, ice makers, generators, ramps and serving carts.

COMPLETING THE PART A ITEMIZED EXPENDITURE LIST 
 
FOR EACH INVOICE BEING SUBMITTED FOR THE CUR RENT REQUEST PERIOD : 
 
1. List the vendor name.   
2. List the invoice number.  If there is none, then leave blank. 
3. Enter a brief description of the item purchased or service provided. 
4. List the date the item or service was actually received by your organization.   
5. List the cost of the items that you are seeking reimbursement for. 
 
After you have listed all invoices for the current request period for the Project Equipment Budget Category, total the amounts 
listed in the “COST” column.  Enter this amount on the “CURRENT EXPENDITURES  CLAIMED” line.  Next, for the first 
disbursement request, compare this amount to the amount listed in the “APPROVED  COSTS” box of the BUDGET at the 
top of the page.  If the “CURRENT EXPENDITURES  CLAIMED” is greater than the amount listed in the “APPROVED  
COSTS” box, then you have exceeded the contract maximum for the budget category and your current expenditures must 
be reduced.  Subtract the “CURRENT  EXPENDITURES  CLAIMED” from the “APPROVED  COSTS” and enter that amount 
on the line marked “AMOUNT IN EXCESS OF BUDGET.” Then, to 	determine the “CURRENT ELIGIBLE  
EXPENDITURES” subtract the “AMOUNT IN  EXCESS” from the “CURRENT  EXPENDITURES  CLAIMED” and enter that 
amount on the “CURRENT  ELIGIBLE  EXPENDITURES” line.  This is the amount of the current expenditures that is eligible 
for reimbursement. 
 
For subsequent requests, after you have calculated the “CURRENT EXPENDITURES CLAIMED” you must add that amount 
to the amount listed on the “CURRENT ELIGIBLE EXPENDITURES” line from the previous disbursement request of the 
previous PART A Form. If the total exceeds the “APPROVED COSTS” of the budget, then once again, the current 
expenditures claimed must be reduced. To determine the amount in excess of the contract maximum, add the “CURRENT  
EXPENDITURES CLAIMED” to the amount listed on the “CURRENT ELIGIBLE EXPENDITURES” line from the previous 
disbursement request of the previous PART A Form and subtract the total from the “APPROVED  COSTS.” Enter this 
amount on the “AMOUNT IN EXCESS OF BUDGET” line.  Then to determine the “CURRENT ELIGIBLE EXPENDITURES,” 
subtract the “AMOUNT IN EXCESS” from the “CURRENT EXPENDITURES CLAIMED” and enter this amount on the 
“CURRENT ELIGIBLE EXPENDITURES” line. 
 
Make sure to attach, in the order listed, copies of the invoices. 
 
Please Note: Make sure you list only the invoices that apply to the Project Equipment Budget Category.
